How alerts are identified

Critical combines a company-adjusted decline of at least 3 points, p < .05, and broad item decline. Watch flags meaningful decline with weaker evidence. Improving requires a significant company-adjusted gain of at least 3 points. Alerts are screening signals, not causal findings.
