## Design an effective Microsoft Viva Glint survey program

`survey-program-design` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `medium`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/customize-program

Short program-design guidance. The article provides principles and links, not the full content of the linked design video, eBook, or discovery workbook.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Template-based or blank programs** (documentation) - Start with a populated template or a blank program and customize to the target population.
   Topics: survey-design, glint-admin. Evidence: `L2`, "prepopulated or blank".
2. **Relevant employee-experience questions** (recommendation) - Question selection should reflect experiences relevant to the people surveyed.
   Topics: survey-design. Evidence: `L4`, "relevant".
3. **Strategic alignment** (recommendation) - Survey content should align with company priorities rather than exist as a disconnected HR exercise.
   Topics: survey-design, leadership. Evidence: `L4`, "strategic priorities".
4. **Candid and actionable feedback** (recommendation) - A good design should make it possible to give honest feedback that can inform action.
   Topics: feedback, action-taking. Evidence: `L4`, "candid and actionable".
5. **Discovery before configuration** (signpost) - The linked workbook helps articulate business needs and information relevant to the listening strategy.
   Topics: employee-listening, work-design. Evidence: `L12`, "critical business information".
6. **Stakeholder agreement** (recommendation) - Align stakeholders before finalizing a program that reflects organizational needs.
   Topics: leadership, survey-design. Evidence: `L9`, "align with your internal stakeholders".
7. **Cadence matched to needs** (recommendation) - Choose cadence in context of other programs and the capacity to act; more frequent cycles can support new habits.
   Topics: survey-design, action-taking. Evidence: `L14`, "Different needs".
8. **Participant fatigue** (recommendation) - Keep surveys short, relevant, and easy and follow them with visible action.
   Topics: employee-listening, survey-design. Evidence: `L17`, "short, relevant".
9. **Leader fatigue** (recommendation) - Use feedback to focus existing work and mutual support rather than adding unrelated demands.
   Topics: leadership, work-design. Evidence: `L19`, "existing efforts".
10. **HR fatigue and scalable resources** (recommendation) - Manager Focus Area resources can reduce HR's burden of interpreting and responding to every request.
   Topics: action-taking, learning-enablement. Evidence: `L21`, "overwhelmed".
