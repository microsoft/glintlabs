## Review and test Viva Glint surveys before launch

`survey-qa` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/survey-qa

Complete launch-readiness checklist and testing workflow. The index condenses many settings into ten operational themes; it is not a replacement for the checklist.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Tenant-wide experience settings** (documentation) - Check company identity, time zones, hierarchy, alert attributes, and communication-channel choices.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L6`, "survey-taking and reporting".
2. **Access and localization** (documentation) - Verify Entra or alternative survey access, supported languages, and comment-analysis language settings before launch.
   Topics: glint-admin, inclusion. Evidence: `L66`, "before a survey launches".
3. **Confidentiality fixed before launch** (documentation) - Confirm rated-score, response-rate, and comment privacy thresholds; these cannot be changed after launch.
   Topics: privacy-confidentiality, glint-admin. Evidence: `L86`, "can't change confidentiality".
4. **Program behavior controls** (documentation) - Check administrators, response windows, suggested actions, nudges, resubmission, team conversations, and raw-export permissions.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L156`, "Raw Survey Responses".
5. **Distribution and schedule accuracy** (documentation) - Confirm included/excluded populations, start dates, frequency, and response or conversation windows.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L169`, "Distribution For This Program".
6. **Questionnaire and translation QA** (documentation) - Validate wording, order, cycle assignment, targeting, display logic, sections, and translated participant messages.
   Topics: survey-design, inclusion. Evidence: `L229`, "Targeting".
7. **Report roles and outcome setup** (documentation) - Review live/phased access, report templates, question access, aggregates, key outcomes, and Copilot/report guidance settings.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L254`, "phased access".
8. **Communications and coaching QA** (documentation) - Verify email/Teams timing, reminders, translations, and interpretation or conversation resources.
   Topics: learning-enablement, glint-admin. Evidence: `L334`, "correct schedule".
9. **Preview and representative testers** (recommendation) - Use report previews and testers covering project stakeholders, languages, access modes, and question logic.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L385`, "two to three weeks".
10. **Isolated test program and defect closure** (documentation) - Duplicate the program for tests, log and resolve feedback, delete the test program, then approve and enable the real launch.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L398`, "always create a copy".
