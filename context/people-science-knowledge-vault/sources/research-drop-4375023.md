## Research Drop: Understanding and Encouraging Effective Manager Behaviors

`research-drop-4375023` | research-drop | reviewed

**Priority:** New catalog; before relevant original-vault resources.

**Source:** https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftvivablog/research-drop-understanding-and-encouraging-effective-manager-behaviors/4375023

February 2025 public article and two figures reviewed. July 2024 US manager panel, n=500, 5+ direct reports. Effective managers self-report always performing at least six of ten behaviors. Classification and comparison are descriptive and partly definition-driven; self-report, tenure, burnout, and engagement associations are not causal.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Also reviewed OCR of 2 public figures; numeric claims cross-checked against written text where available. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

**Study-family links:** manager-action-july-2024

1. **Three manager-behavior domains** (framework) - The framework organizes ten behaviors into coaching/mentoring, cohesion, and enabling high performance.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, leadership. Evidence: `L19`, "three main themes".
2. **Career development and strategic connection** (recommendation) - Coaching includes growth conversations, regular one-to-ones, and linking daily work to strategy.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, learning-enablement, meaningful-work, growth-development. Evidence: `L21`, "stretch assignments".
3. **Cohesion through voice and recognition** (recommendation) - Welcoming differences, recognizing work, and sharing information support connected teams.
   Topics: psychological-safety, inclusion, connection. Evidence: `L23`, "variety of perspectives".
4. **Clarity, prioritization, and barrier removal** (recommendation) - Managers enable performance by clarifying work, resolving obstacles, and encouraging experimentation.
   Topics: work-design, manager-effectiveness, role-clarity, empowerment. Evidence: `L27`, "removing potential barriers".
5. **Consistency remains difficult** (finding) - Only one in five meet the study's effective-manager threshold, while often performing a behavior is much more common than always doing it.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, methods. Evidence: `L44`, "1 in 5".
6. **One-to-ones are especially hard to sustain** (finding) - Consistent individual meetings are the hardest behavior for both manager groups.
   Topics: feedback, manager-effectiveness. Evidence: `L46`, "most challenging behavior".
7. **Priorities differ by job level** (finding) - Executives emphasize strategy/coaching, directors cohesion, and first-line managers execution; teams may still need a broader mix.
   Topics: leadership, manager-effectiveness. Evidence: `L56`, "situational awareness".
8. **Manager engagement co-occurs with effectiveness** (finding) - Effective managers are more frequently engaged in this sample; the article proposes a reinforcing team dynamic.
   Topics: engagement, manager-effectiveness. Evidence: `L62`, "Ninety-three".
9. **Burnout constrains management capacity** (finding) - Burnout indicators are more common among typical managers; organizations should support managers' own working conditions.
   Topics: wellbeing, manager-effectiveness. Evidence: `L66`, "59%".
10. **Experience and ongoing development** (recommendation) - Longer manager tenure is associated with consistency but can include blind spots; development is relevant at every tenure.
   Topics: learning-enablement, manager-effectiveness, growth-development. Evidence: `L71`, "blind spots".
