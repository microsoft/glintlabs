## User roles in Viva Pulse

`user-roles-in-viva-pulse` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `medium`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/pulse/setup-admin-access/roles-viva-pulse

Complete Pulse role page, including conditional customization and licensing links. License lists are time-sensitive and must be checked live before procurement or access decisions.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Multiple roles per user** (documentation) - A user may hold author, provider, and administrator roles in combination.
   Topics: pulse-admin. Evidence: `L2`, "one or more".
2. **Author audiences and self-inclusion** (documentation) - Authors can request feedback from reports, team members, other organizational users, and themselves.
   Topics: pulse-admin, employee-listening. Evidence: `L7`, "add themselves".
3. **Template-based personalization** (documentation) - Authors start from research-backed templates and personalize to team needs.
   Topics: survey-design, pulse-admin. Evidence: `L7`, "research-backed".
4. **Adding custom questions is conditional** (documentation) - Authors may add questions only when their organization enables that capability.
   Topics: pulse-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L8`, "enabled".
5. **Editing stock questions is conditional** (documentation) - Updating, reordering, or deleting stock questions depends on an organizational setting.
   Topics: pulse-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L11`, "editing stock questions".
6. **Autosaved drafts** (documentation) - Unsent requests retain work so authors can return later.
   Topics: pulse-admin. Evidence: `L14`, "automatically saved".
7. **Closed and thresholded results** (documentation) - Authors see results only after closure and sufficient responses and can share them for discussion.
   Topics: privacy-confidentiality, feedback. Evidence: `L15`, "minimum number".
8. **Licensed sending rights** (documentation) - Sending a request requires a qualifying Pulse license; use the current linked licensing list.
   Topics: pulse-admin. Evidence: `L16`, "valid license".
9. **Provider tenant membership** (documentation) - Providers receive requests as members of the organization's Microsoft Entra tenant and supply aggregate-report input.
   Topics: pulse-admin, employee-listening. Evidence: `L20`, "Microsoft Entra tenant".
10. **Administrator configuration and assignment** (documentation) - Licensed Pulse admins configure the experience and are assigned through Entra by an appropriately authorized administrator.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L24`, "appropriately authorized".
