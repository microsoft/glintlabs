## Understand Viva Glint programs and cycles

`understand-viva-glint-programs-and-cycles` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/program-cycle-edits

Full recurring-program/cycle editing documentation. Unlinking changes configuration inheritance, not historical reporting, and relinking is not currently supported.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Programs as configuration containers** (documentation) - Programs hold settings shared across a sequence of recurring surveys.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L5`, "container".
2. **Cycles as timed surveys** (documentation) - A cycle is a specific survey occasion, normally named with its launch month and year.
   Topics: employee-listening, glint-admin. Evidence: `L7`, "specific point in time".
3. **Default linked inheritance** (documentation) - Linked cycles receive program-level configuration updates.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L9`, "automatically apply".
4. **Cycle-level customization** (documentation) - Editing one cycle allows local changes without altering later surveys.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L11`, "only affect that survey".
5. **Prefer program-level changes** (recommendation) - Use the program to configure future repeated surveys instead of unnecessarily editing every cycle.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L13`, "avoid major adjustments".
6. **Unlinking stops future propagation** (documentation) - Once a cycle is edited and unlinked, subsequent program updates no longer flow into it.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L18`, "no longer flow".
7. **No supported relink operation** (documentation) - The documentation warns that edited cycles cannot currently be relinked.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L19`, "doesn't currently support".
8. **Approved-cycle edit workflow** (documentation) - Select an approved survey's upcoming or live cycle and confirm the unlink warning before editing.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L35`, "any edits unlink".
9. **Distribution, questions, and communications** (documentation) - These are the configuration sections identified for local cycle edits.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L40`, "Distribution".
10. **Reporting continuity and live reminders** (documentation) - Unlinking does not itself affect reporting or trends; live reminder changes require cycle-level updates.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L50`, "don't affect reporting".
