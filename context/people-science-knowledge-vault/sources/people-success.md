## Reimagining People Success in the age of AI

`people-success` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `high`.

**Source:** https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftvivablog/it%E2%80%99s-a-brave-new-work-world-reimagining-people-success-in-the-age-of-ai/4428208

Published synthesis of 2022-2025 customer survey and voluntary-exit data (6.8M responses, 235+ companies); correlations and observed attrition comparisons, not causal effects. AI applications mix research with recommendations.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Also reviewed OCR of 13 public figures; numeric claims cross-checked against written text where available. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Six People Success needs** (framework) - Purpose, growth, clarity, empowerment, connection, and wellbeing organize the workplace conditions relevant to engagement.
   Topics: people-science, engagement. Evidence: `L14`, "Purpose, Growth, Clarity".
2. **Meaningful work and staying** (finding) - Meaningful work has the strongest reported voluntary-exit association in this analysis; the comparison does not establish that adding purpose prevents exits.
   Topics: retention, work-design, meaningful-work. Evidence: `L58`, "3.8x less likely".
3. **Growth and career opportunity** (finding) - Learning opportunities and perceived career prospects are strongly associated with engagement and retention measures.
   Topics: retention, growth-development. Evidence: `L90`, "2.8x less likely".
4. **Role clarity and priorities** (finding) - Clear expectations, priorities, and useful feedback are associated with staying; AI-related work changes make clarity especially relevant.
   Topics: work-design, retention, role-clarity. Evidence: `L122`, "all three factors".
5. **Human performance coaching** (recommendation) - Manager feedback remains a distinct interpersonal responsibility even where AI assists with prioritization or coaching.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, feedback. Evidence: `L141`, "human manager-direct report".
6. **Empowerment through resources** (finding) - Resources, process improvement, and encouragement to try new approaches feature prominently among engagement correlates.
   Topics: engagement, work-design, empowerment. Evidence: `L161`, "Continuous Process Improvements".
7. **Belonging and inclusion** (finding) - Belonging is a persistent retention correlate; the article proposes accessible AI communication tools as one way to support inclusion.
   Topics: inclusion, retention, connection. Evidence: `L212`, "top five drivers".
8. **Care, balance, and wellbeing** (finding) - Feeling cared for, supported, and able to balance work and life have gained importance in the article's attrition comparisons.
   Topics: wellbeing, retention. Evidence: `L248`, "increased in power".
9. **People-centric AI change** (recommendation) - Provide experimentation time, role-specific use cases, transparent communication, and psychological safety rather than treating AI as task automation alone.
   Topics: ai-adoption, change-management. Evidence: `L93`, "People Science research".
10. **Limits of the research design** (method) - Engagement correlations and favorable-versus-unfavorable exit comparisons use different methods and eligibility rules; retain these distinctions when quoting results.
   Topics: methods, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L304`, "Attrition Multipliers".
