## Deck — Understanding employee reactions to AI

`deck-understanding-employee-reactions-to-ai` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `high`.

**Source:** https://adoption.microsoft.com/files/viva/Microsoft-Viva_Think-like-a-People-Scientist_Mar2025.pdf

Complete 33-page public March 2025 webinar deck. Uses the April 2024 AI Readiness study, with different analysis samples (1,800 overall; 1,389 profile analysis; 1,155 AI users for RIVA). Same study as the readiness eBook and AI Profiles Drop, not independent corroboration.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete extracted text of the public 33-page PDF. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

**Study-family links:** ai-readiness-april-2024

1. **Change fatigue and engagement** (finding) - The deck contrasts declining willingness to support change with greater AI-change eagerness among engaged employees.
   Topics: change-management, engagement. Evidence: `p5`, "losing patience".
2. **Unprovided AI tools** (finding) - Employees often use AI tools their organization has not supplied, raising enablement and governance questions.
   Topics: ai-adoption, ai-governance. Evidence: `p9`, "not provided".
3. **Executive sponsorship and investment** (finding) - Executives' favorable expectations suggest formal sponsorship and rollout are relevant change-management opportunities.
   Topics: leadership, ai-strategy. Evidence: `p10`, "worth the investment".
4. **Burnout and capacity for change** (finding) - Change-related stress and strain vary with the number of burnout risk factors in the study.
   Topics: wellbeing, change-management. Evidence: `p11`, "burnout risk factors".
5. **Data-derived transformation profiles** (method) - Profiles combine engagement, prior change experience, and AI sentiment; they are not fixed personality types.
   Topics: ai-adoption, methods. Evidence: `p15`, "Change Experience".
6. **Five distinct employee profiles** (framework) - Multipliers, Advocates, Persuadables, AI Skeptics, and Change Pessimists differ in readiness patterns.
   Topics: ai-adoption, engagement. Evidence: `p17`, "Change Pessimists".
7. **Profile differences in AI usage** (finding) - Usage-frequency patterns differ markedly across profiles in the cross-sectional study.
   Topics: ai-adoption, methods. Evidence: `p24`, "AI Usage Frequency".
8. **Differentiated contributions** (recommendation) - Champions, use-case sharers, realistic challengers, risk spotters, and change-experience critics can each contribute to transformation.
   Topics: ai-adoption, inclusion. Evidence: `p25`, "contribute".
9. **Tailored change support** (recommendation) - Match support to employees' starting points, from experimentation opportunities to safeguards, feedback channels, and training resources.
   Topics: change-management, ai-skills. Evidence: `p26`, "Support this profile".
10. **Realized value and nonjudgmental segmentation** (recommendation) - RIVA outcomes vary across profiles, but the guidance emphasizes meeting people where they are and recognizing profiles can change.
   Topics: ai-value, inclusion. Evidence: `p31`, "not static".
