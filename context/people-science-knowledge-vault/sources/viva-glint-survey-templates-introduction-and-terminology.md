## Viva Glint survey templates — introduction & terminology

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `medium`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/templates-intro

Full Question Library terminology and referent guidance. Identical item wording does not guarantee equivalent scale, program type, or interpretation.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Validated library items** (documentation) - Standard templates draw on People Science-tested items stored in the Question Library.
   Topics: survey-design, methods. Evidence: `L6`, "tests and validates".
2. **Item names and unique IDs** (documentation) - Question IDs distinguish items that may share a display name or identical wording.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L25`, "different Question ID".
3. **Scale and program compatibility** (documentation) - Check rating scale and program type before selecting between apparently identical items.
   Topics: survey-design, methods. Evidence: `L53`, "matches your survey".
4. **Customer-specific item copies** (documentation) - Adding even an unedited standard item creates a customer-specific Custom version in the library.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L57`, "customer-specific version".
5. **Rating, open text, and options** (documentation) - Question types support scaled responses, written feedback, or selectable choices.
   Topics: survey-design. Evidence: `L66`, "Question Type".
6. **Referents define who is rated** (framework) - Referents specify a manager, team, company, or another population to keep in mind when responding.
   Topics: survey-design, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L63`, "group or population".
7. **Team versus teams and coworkers** (documentation) - The article distinguishes one's own team from broader teams and coworkers across the organization.
   Topics: survey-design, work-design. Evidence: `L85`, "more generally".
8. **Manager, leaders, and company referents** (documentation) - Direct manager, executive leadership, management chain, and whole-company experiences are not interchangeable.
   Topics: leadership, survey-design. Evidence: `L93`, "management chain".
9. **Experience-first items without referents** (recommendation) - Where an experience has several influences, an unreferenced item can be relevant across employees and support comparison.
   Topics: survey-design, benchmarks. Evidence: `L118`, "experience itself".
10. **Tags, actions, and comment interpretation** (documentation) - Item metadata links response options and action templates; comment analytics help connect broad themes with sentiment.
   Topics: action-taking, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L121`, "comment data".
