## How Microsoft People Science drives Viva Glint programs

`template-research` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/template-research

Published explanation of template construction, drawing on 350M survey responses. Reported relationships are associations; a templated survey is not automatically composed only of validated items.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Research-led template construction** (method) - People Science identifies engagement-related experiences and translates driver themes into survey templates.
   Topics: people-science, survey-design. Evidence: `L7`, "350 million".
2. **Core needs with local variation** (framework) - The six People Success Elements summarize needs, while their expression differs across companies.
   Topics: people-science, engagement. Evidence: `L11`, "one company to another".
3. **Purpose and recommendation** (finding) - Purpose is associated with greater willingness to recommend one's employer in the cited research.
   Topics: engagement, work-design, meaningful-work. Evidence: `L17`, "sense of purpose".
4. **Clarity and burnout** (finding) - The page contrasts reported burnout by role-clarity status; the comparison does not establish a causal effect.
   Topics: wellbeing, work-design, role-clarity. Evidence: `L19`, "role clarity".
5. **Growth and staying intentions** (finding) - Perceived growth opportunities are associated with intentions to remain, not a guaranteed reduction in observed exits.
   Topics: retention, learning-enablement, growth-development. Evidence: `L21`, "plan to be working".
6. **Empowerment and happiness** (finding) - Empowerment is linked to happiness at work in the published comparison.
   Topics: engagement, work-design, empowerment. Evidence: `L23`, "empowered at work".
7. **Belonging and engagement** (finding) - A strong sense of belonging is associated with greater engagement.
   Topics: inclusion, engagement, connection. Evidence: `L25`, "strong sense of belonging".
8. **Care, flexibility, and wellbeing** (finding) - Feeling cared for and priorities around flexibility and balance are part of the wellbeing rationale.
   Topics: wellbeing, inclusion. Evidence: `L27`, "feel cared for".
9. **Recommended versus other drivers** (documentation) - Core drivers are a curated shortlist; additional drivers may also be relevant to the intended outcome.
   Topics: survey-design, methods. Evidence: `L37`, "short list".
10. **Validated items versus templated surveys** (documentation) - Validated items carry added research rigor, but the existence of a template does not guarantee every included item is validated.
   Topics: methods, survey-design. Evidence: `L43`, "nonvalidated".
