## Research Drop: Tracking the Rise of Perceived AI Value at Work

`research-drop-4511230` | research-drop | reviewed

**Priority:** New catalog; before relevant original-vault resources.

**Source:** https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftvivablog/research-drop-tracking-the-rise-of-perceived-ai-value-at-work/4511230

April 2026 public article and six figures reviewed. Three independent samples of AI-using managers/ICs: April 2024 global n=910, July 2025 global n=811, November 2025 US-only n=610; total n=2,331. No repeated participants, changing geography and composition, small subgroups, and some missing departments mean directional snapshots, not within-person growth. RIVA is mean item favorability, not a transformed mean or financial ROI.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Also reviewed OCR of 6 public figures; numeric claims cross-checked against written text where available. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

**Study-family links:** ai-readiness-april-2024, agentic-teaming-trust-july-2025, ai-outcomes-november-2025

1. **Measure value as well as adoption** (framework) - RIVA captures perceived quality, speed, complexity, productivity, decisions, and stress benefits that usage counts do not.
   Topics: ai-value, employee-listening. Evidence: `L19`, "six survey items".
2. **Directional increase across separate samples** (finding) - Overall favorability rises nine percentage points across the three snapshots; no employee is tracked longitudinally.
   Topics: ai-value, methods. Evidence: `L28`, "9-percentage".
3. **Usage and value are intertwined** (finding) - More frequent users report greater value; the article explicitly treats the proposed reinforcing loop as noncausal.
   Topics: ai-adoption, ai-value. Evidence: `L40`, "not causal".
4. **Population composition can shift the aggregate** (finding) - Value within usage groups is fairly stable while more respondents fall into frequent-use groups, complicating interpretation of overall movement.
   Topics: methods, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L43`, "population".
5. **Manager and contributor paths differ** (finding) - Managers and ICs show different directional patterns and a temporarily narrowed gap; workflow explanations are hypotheses.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, inclusion. Evidence: `L50`, "plausible interpretation".
6. **Organization-size differences** (finding) - Respondents at 1,000-4,999-employee organizations report more value than larger groups; proposed agility mechanisms are not isolated causal effects.
   Topics: ai-value, work-design. Evidence: `L60`, "4,999".
7. **Industry context shapes value routes** (finding) - Sector differences are directional and affected by workflow, governance, and small subgroup samples rather than a universal ranking.
   Topics: work-design, ai-governance. Evidence: `L68`, "directional signals".
8. **Functional proximity and HR's later rise** (finding) - IT remains high while HR's later snapshot rises toward it; the article proposes proximity to transformation as one explanation.
   Topics: hr-it, ai-value. Evidence: `L74`, "almost on par".
9. **Value and professional uncertainty coexist** (commentary) - Engineering/R&D patterns illustrate that perceived benefits need not eliminate anxiety about role identity and future relevance.
   Topics: wellbeing, change-management. Evidence: `L82`, "professional uncertainty".
10. **Design for distributed, sustained benefit** (recommendation) - Invest in workflow integration, clear guardrails, trust, and evolving work practices so value does not concentrate only among early insiders.
   Topics: inclusion, ai-strategy. Evidence: `L84`, "intentional design".

## Shared research context

### ai-readiness-april-2024

April 2024 global AI readiness research, n=1,800 overall; profile, function, frontline, and training analyses use different subsets. Repeated articles/decks and the later n=910 snapshot are related analyses, not independent replications.

### agentic-teaming-trust-july-2025

July 2025 global panel, n=1,800 overall (819 leaders, 520 managers, 461 ICs). Chapter 4 and monthly drops use differing subsets/constructs; later n=811 managers/ICs form one of the repeated-publication snapshots. Multiple publications are not multiple independent studies.

### ai-outcomes-november-2025

US-only November 2025 sample of 610 AI-using managers/ICs, reused by the April and May 2026 articles. It is not a repeated-person follow-up of the two earlier global samples.
