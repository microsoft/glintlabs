## Propel action-taking through conversations with Viva Glint

`propel-action-taking` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `high`.

**Source:** https://adoption.microsoft.com/files/viva/glint/Propel-action-taking-through-conversations-with-Microsoft-Viva-Glint.pdf

Complete 15-page May 2023 guide blending customer observations, behavioral-science rationale, and practical advice. Reported engagement differences are not randomized causal effects.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete extracted text of the public 15-page PDF. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Credibility depends on visible action** (finding) - Belief that feedback leads to action is associated with engagement and leadership confidence.
   Topics: action-taking, engagement. Evidence: `p2`, "7x".
2. **Distributed ownership, not HR overload** (framework) - Delayed manager access and unclear responsibility undermine action; teams and leaders share ownership.
   Topics: action-taking, leadership. Evidence: `p3`, "Ownership of action".
3. **Manager support and enablement** (recommendation) - Managers need tools, skills, and help with difficult feedback rather than being expected to fix everything alone.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, learning-enablement. Evidence: `p4`, "Support and enablement".
4. **Simple agile action** (recommendation) - Replace elaborate static plans with timely feedback, one manageable focus, and iterative course correction.
   Topics: action-taking, change-management. Evidence: `p6`, "one impactful shift".
5. **Conversations sustain momentum** (framework) - Recurring conversations support shared understanding, collaborative ownership, and accountability even without a new survey.
   Topics: feedback, action-taking. Evidence: `p7`, "Conversations are self-correcting".
6. **ACT conversation structure** (framework) - Acknowledge current experience, Collaborate on direction, and Take one step forward.
   Topics: action-taking, feedback. Evidence: `p8`, "Acknowledge".
7. **Safety and strengths in discussion** (recommendation) - Open discussion, positive progress stories, and shared prioritization help teams build trust without treating every survey item as a separate task.
   Topics: psychological-safety, action-taking. Evidence: `p9`, "Create trust".
8. **Start, stop, continue and commitments** (recommendation) - Consider what to stop as well as what to add, agree individual commitments, and plan the next check-in.
   Topics: action-taking, work-design. Evidence: `p10`, "start, stop, continue".
9. **Business priorities and existing meetings** (recommendation) - Connect feedback to the next three to six months of team objectives and use established meeting rhythms.
   Topics: action-taking, work-design. Evidence: `p12`, "3-6 months".
10. **Four manager roles and impact stories** (recommendation) - Managers coach, facilitate, remove roadblocks, and connect; communicate meaningful changes rather than merely activity counts.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, leadership. Evidence: `p13`, "Roadblock Remover".
