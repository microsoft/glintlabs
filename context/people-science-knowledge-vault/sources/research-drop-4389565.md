## Research Drop: Investing in HR & IT Collaboration to Drive Successful AI Transformation

`research-drop-4389565` | research-drop | reviewed

**Priority:** New catalog; before relevant original-vault resources.

**Source:** https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftvivablog/research-drop-investing-in-hr--it-collaboration-to-drive-successful-ai-transform/4389565

March 2025 public article and two figures reviewed. HR/IT subset n=413 of April 2024 AI-readiness panel. Same evidence family as the HR/IT webinar deck and readiness eBook. Sponsorship and RIVA are associated, not randomized; examples such as ESS illustrate use cases rather than establish general measured ROI.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Also reviewed OCR of 2 public figures; numeric claims cross-checked against written text where available. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

**Study-family links:** ai-readiness-april-2024

1. **Complementary human and technical expertise** (framework) - IT manages systems while HR contributes workforce, skills, and employee-change expertise.
   Topics: hr-it, ai-strategy. Evidence: `L9`, "shouldn’t be operating in silos".
2. **HR and IT differ in AI enthusiasm** (finding) - Both functions are broadly positive, but IT respondents report stronger optimism and anticipated organizational benefits.
   Topics: hr-it, ai-adoption. Evidence: `L20`, "55%".
3. **HR applications beyond transactions** (commentary) - The article uses employee self-service as an example of shifting capacity toward more relational HR work.
   Topics: hr-it, work-design. Evidence: `L23`, "relational tasks".
4. **Security and skills as shared goals** (recommendation) - Data protection and training combine complementary IT and HR strengths.
   Topics: hr-it, ai-governance, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L26`, "shared priority".
5. **Unequal sponsored access** (finding) - All/most-tool sponsorship is reported by 72% of IT respondents versus 59% of HR respondents.
   Topics: ai-adoption, inclusion. Evidence: `L33`, "59%".
6. **Sponsored access and individual value** (finding) - Higher-access respondents in both functions report more benefits across the six RIVA outcomes.
   Topics: ai-value, hr-it. Evidence: `L42`, "six RIVA outcomes".
7. **Transformation beyond task speed** (finding) - Decision quality and stress benefits are harder to realize than faster task completion.
   Topics: ai-value, work-design. Evidence: `L45`, "more subtle benefits".
8. **Equitable training and resourcing** (recommendation) - HR needs comparable learning opportunities, time, money, and staff to participate strategically.
   Topics: ai-skills, inclusion. Evidence: `L54`, "lack of resources".
9. **Cross-functional experimentation communities** (recommendation) - Shared language, peer forums, and joint taskforces can help avoid departmental AI silos.
   Topics: hr-it, learning-enablement. Evidence: `L69`, "cross-pollination".
10. **Bring employee voice into measurement** (recommendation) - HR can help co-design suitable automation and combine sentiment with technology behavior to assess rollout.
   Topics: employee-listening, ai-value. Evidence: `L73`, "employee sentiment".
