# Platform Screenshot Interpretation context

Use the [skill entry](../SKILL.md) for user-authorized Viva Glint screenshots,
dashboard views, and supplied aggregate results. No installation, local scripts,
survey export, or manifest is required for ordinary screenshot interpretation.

## Read in order

1. Follow [results exploration](results-exploration.md) to retain visible scope,
   explain patterns cautiously, and choose one useful verification.
2. Read the shared research library's [context entry](../../people-science-knowledge-vault/references/README.md),
   [source priority](../../people-science-knowledge-vault/references/source-priority.md),
   [public-use contract](../../people-science-knowledge-vault/references/public-use.md),
   and [research workflow](../../people-science-knowledge-vault/references/research-workflow.md).
   Retrieve applicable original public sources before substantive interpretation.
3. Before named report/view recommendations, click paths, or browser navigation,
   also load [Glint navigation](glint-navigation.md).

Keep every repository document on the same repository and ref as the supplied
skill link, including the shared research library. Do not load the research
skill as a new task for each article; this skill retains the investigation.
The [required-reference gate](../SKILL.md#required-reference-gate) applies to all
required bodies, reuse, recovery, and conditional navigation.

The research corpus, source priorities, publication permissions, and source
verification remain maintained once in Knowledge Vault. Do not duplicate its
catalog, topic/source pages, or publication bodies here. General research-only
questions belong to [People Science Knowledge Vault](../../people-science-knowledge-vault/SKILL.md).

For interpretation of analysis artifacts, shared secondary guardrails live in
`../../../references/general/`, particularly
[interpretation guardrails](../../../references/general/interpretation-guardrails.md)
and [privacy guidance](../../../references/general/privacy-and-minimum-n.md).
Use `analyze-survey` for computations and artifact QA. Its analysis thresholds
do not establish a universal platform reporting threshold or license suppressed
screen interpretation; verify current product rules and respect suppression.
