## Set up a Microsoft Viva Glint Recurring survey

`set-up-a-microsoft-viva-glint-recurring-survey` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/program-summary-overview

Task-specific recurring-survey setup page. It explicitly supports adding cycles to a blank recurring template, unlike an older statement in the general program-types page; retain this discrepancy.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete freshly retrieved public Microsoft Learn article text reviewed, including substantive tables. Sign-in notices and page chrome excluded as evidence. Linked pages, courses, downloads, recordings and image-only details were not reviewed; no refreshed comprehensive figure or media review is claimed.

1. **Regular topic tracking** (documentation) - Recurring surveys measure sentiment on themes such as engagement, productivity, manager effectiveness and culture at regular intervals.
   Topics: survey-design, employee-listening. Evidence: `L19`, "Use Microsoft Viva Glint Recurring surveys to measure employee sentiment on topics that you want regular feedback on over time. Recurring surveys help".
2. **Cycle edits irreversibly unlink** (documentation) - Editing a survey cycle unlinks it from the overall survey program; Viva Glint does not currently support relinking. Plan program-level changes before making local cycle edits.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L22`, "Edits made to survey cycles  unlink cycles from the overall survey program . Viva Glint doesn't currently support relinking cycles to their survey pro".
3. **Blank recurring template supports cycles** (documentation) - Create a recurring program from a template or blank. Contrary to the general program-types overview, this task-specific page explicitly supports multiple cycles in the blank recurring template; add at least one cycle and at least one question to a cycle.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L28`, "The blank recurring survey template allows you to create multiple survey cycles.".
4. **Program language and confidentiality** (documentation) - Program Setup defines basic languages, optional features and confidentiality settings; these must be configured rather than inferred from the chosen template.
   Topics: glint-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L34`, "Define basics like languages, optional features, and confidentiality settings.".
5. **Distribution and recurring schedule** (documentation) - Distribution includes/excludes invitees through Distribution Lists or User Roles; Schedule sets the next start date and recurrence for future surveys.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L36`, "Select Distribution Lists or User Roles to include in or exclude from the survey invite list.".
6. **Question and reporting setup** (documentation) - Questions configures introduction, survey items and thank-you text. Reporting determines which roles access results and the key reporting views.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L40`, "Add survey introduction text, select questions, and add a survey thank you message.".
7. **Communications and coaching setup** (documentation) - Communications customizes and schedules survey messages. Coaching configures interpretation guidance and Team Conversations content when enabled.
   Topics: glint-admin, learning-enablement. Evidence: `L46`, "Confirm or customize content that helps users interpret results on their dashboards and use Team Conversations (when enabled).".
8. **Preview and launch gates** (documentation) - Setup section checkmarks indicate completion. Preview after each section, review setup before launch, then enable and launch the survey; detailed linked procedures are separate assets.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L50`, "Preview your survey  after completing each setup section.".
9. **Prelaunch and closing reminders** (documentation) - The page suggests a customized introductory email one to two days before launch and a calendar alert for a full-list reminder 72 hours before close; these are communication recommendations, not automatically scheduled guarantees.
   Topics: learning-enablement, glint-admin. Evidence: `L52`, "1-2 days before your survey launch,  customize this email template and send it to your Distribution Lists.".
10. **Post-close organizational messages** (documentation) - Suggested organizational follow-through is a closure/next-steps message one day after close and results/feedback expectations three to four days after close.
   Topics: action-taking, learning-enablement. Evidence: `L58`, "1 day after survey close:  Announce that the survey is closed and explain what's next".
11. **Post-close manager preparation** (documentation) - Suggested manager communications cover results and confidentiality one day after close and effective conversations five to seven days after close.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, action-taking, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L61`, "1 day after survey close:  Survey results and confidentiality standard".
