## Viva Glint survey templates — introduction & terminology

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `medium`.

Full Question Library terminology and referent guidance. Identical item wording does not guarantee equivalent scale, program type, or interpretation.

1. **Question Library and item identity** (documentation) - The People Success database in the admin Question Library contains questions and statements measuring distinct experiences. Item Name and Question ID distinguish items in the database; identical wording is not sufficient identification.
   Topics: survey-design, glint-admin. Evidence: `L20`, "The Microsoft Viva Glint People Success database contains survey items - questions and statements - that each measure a distinct employee experience. ".
2. **Check scale and program before reuse** (documentation) - Identically phrased items can belong to different templates or frames of reference. Compare rating scale and program type; in the Career Goals example, the 7-point and 5-point versions have different IDs, so choose the scale matching the survey.
   Topics: survey-design, benchmarks. Evidence: `L26`, "An item may appear more than once in the Question Library. This scenario occurs because the identically phrased item is used for different templates o".
3. **Unedited additions become Custom copies** (documentation) - Adding even an unedited standard Viva Glint item to a program creates a customer-specific Question Library version tagged Custom. Custom status therefore does not by itself prove the wording was edited.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L48`, "Even adding an unedited standard Viva Glint item to a survey program converts it to  Custom  status because the item becomes your customer-specific ve".
4. **Item types and response options** (documentation) - Rating items use 5- or 7-point scales in this terminology guide, open-ended items collect text, and Options items collect single- or multiple-choice selections. Option Text lists choices and Tags identify library items; this is not an exhaustive rule for every Glint metric scale.
   Topics: survey-design. Evidence: `L58`, "Rating items  use a 5- or 7-point scale. Open ended items  are items that require text responses.    Options  are items that require a selection of si".
5. **Action-template metadata** (documentation) - The Action Plan Template field identifies action content aligned to an item. Default denotes generic content; an empty field means no aligned action-plan content.
   Topics: action-taking, survey-design. Evidence: `L60`, "The action plan content aligned to this item. If shown as "default," the item is using the generic template. If empty, this item has no Action Plan co".
6. **Referent specifies the population rated** (documentation) - Referents identify whom the respondent should consider. My team means the respondent's own department/manager group, teams is broader, and coworkers may include people outside that team. Direct manager, executive leadership or management chain, company, and industry-specific roles are different referents.
   Topics: survey-design, methods. Evidence: `L54`, "The group or population (Manager, Coworkers, Company, etc.) a respondent should think about when rating an item".
7. **Referent customization changes item identity** (documentation) - A referent can be customized to the actual name of the person or group rated. The resulting wording appears as a Custom item; changing the rated population is substantively different from leaving the standard experience unchanged.
   Topics: survey-design, glint-admin. Evidence: `L82`, "With all referents, you can customize the item by inserting the actual name of who is to be rated.".
8. **Prefer experience-first items when appropriate** (documentation) - Unless a topic is predominantly experienced through a particular referent, the guide recommends measuring the experience itself. Nonreferent items accommodate multiple influences, support relevance across employees, and enable external-benchmark and overall-company comparisons.
