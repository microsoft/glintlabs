## Viva Glint programs, survey types, and templates

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `medium`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/start/program-types-templates

1. **Engagement definition and purpose** (documentation) - Engagement is cognitive, emotional and behavioral investment toward positive organizational outcomes. The overview describes correlations with satisfaction, commitment, motivation and attrition, and positions programs around conversations and action, not causal guarantees.
   Topics: engagement, action-taking. Evidence: `L21`, "Engagement is the degree to which employees invest their cognitive, emotional, and behavioral energies toward positive organizational outcomes.  Viva ".
2. **Recurring comparisons and cadence** (documentation) - Recurring surveys run on a scheduled cadence and support comparisons and trends between cycles; they can collect feedback on special topics.
   Topics: survey-design, employee-listening. Evidence: `L30`, "Recurring  surveys allow data to be compared between cycles and for  trend  to be established. Recurring surveys are set to go live at a scheduled cad".
3. **Lifecycle event triggers** (documentation) - Lifecycle surveys automatically send based on hire or end dates, measuring key moments rather than a common recurring calendar.
   Topics: survey-design, glint-admin. Evidence: `L33`, "Measure the employee experience during key moments in the employee journey. These surveys are considered  trigger events  because they automatically s".
4. **Ad hoc feedback** (documentation) - Ad hoc surveys measure an out-of-the-ordinary issue at an impactful time.
   Topics: survey-design. Evidence: `L36`, "Measure something out of the ordinary, at an impactful time.".
5. **Always-On feedback** (documentation) - Always-On surveys allow suggestions and feedback whenever relevant, distinct from scheduled or event-triggered listening.
   Topics: employee-listening, survey-design. Evidence: `L39`, "Provide feedback and suggestions anytime it feels relevant.".
6. **Admin cadence and strategic alignment** (documentation) - Admins set cadence and may run surveys simultaneously when appropriate. Design the overall listening strategy around organizational goals and align internal stakeholders rather than choosing programs in isolation.
   Topics: employee-listening, survey-design. Evidence: `L42`, "Admins set the cadence for Viva Glint programs. Surveys can run simultaneously, when appropriate.  Learn about survey fatigue .".
7. **Blank-template cycle discrepancy** (documentation) - This overview states that blank templates do not support adding cycles. The task-specific recurring setup and Questions setup pages explicitly support adding cycles to a blank recurring program; preserve the discrepancy and use the specific recurring instructions for that task.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L49`, "Viva Glint offers prescribed survey templates and a blank template option for each type of program. Template programs can be set to recur (or cycle). ".
8. **Engagement baseline recommendation** (documentation) - The Engagement template measures People Success Elements drivers. Quarterly cycles are recommended as a solid baseline and beneficial introduction, not a mandatory cadence for every organization.
   Topics: engagement, survey-design. Evidence: `L59`, "Recurring: Quarterly cycles create a solid engagement baseline. These surveys are a beneficial first introduction to Viva Glint programs.".
9. **Onboarding and exit populations** (documentation) - Onboarding targets new hires and recommends at least two check-ins at selected points such as 30, 60 or 90 days. Exit targets departing employees to understand departure reasons and organizational perceptions; both are automatically triggered.
   Topics: survey-design, retention. Evidence: `L65`, "Automatically triggered. Consider having at least two Onboarding surveys at specific points in time (for example, 30 days, 60 days, and/or 90 days)".
10. **Copilot Impact versus productivity listening** (documentation) - The Copilot Impact special-topic template is described as measuring impact through comment summarization for AI-adoption and ROI planning. The separate Productivity template addresses experiences supporting productivity and performance; this listing does not establish audited financial ROI.
   Topics: ai-adoption, ai-value, survey-design. Evidence: `L76`, "Measures the impact of Microsoft Copilot in Viva Glint through comment summarization data. Enable IT and business leaders to plan AI adoption and meas".
11. **Culture and inclusion purposes** (documentation) - Culture surveys examine People Success Elements and are recommended to recur according to organizational change, with senior-leadership results. Diversity and Inclusion surveys examine sentiments needed for belonging and target manager/team results.
   Topics: survey-design, inclusion, change-management. Evidence: `L88`, "Measures aspects of the Microsoft People Success Elements to learn what drives people and organizational success.".
12. **Manager versus team effectiveness** (documentation) - Manager Effectiveness measures perceptions of direct-manager behaviors; Team Effectiveness identifies the team's distinctive dynamics and improvement opportunities. Both are listed as recurring special-topic surveys.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, survey-design. Evidence: `L106`, "Assesses employee perceptions of how their direct managers demonstrate key behaviors related to their effectiveness".
13. **Healthcare-specific programs** (documentation) - Patient Safety assesses patient-safety culture and is described as Leapfrog-approved. RN Excellence meets Registered Nurse Satisfaction requirements for the Magnet Recognition program; these are specialized healthcare templates, not generic engagement certifications.
   Topics: survey-design, glint-admin. Evidence: `L100`, "Assesses the culture of patient safety in a valid, reliable, and straightforward way. Leapfrog-approved.".
14. **Distress listening** (documentation) - The recurring Distress template measures employee sentiment when societal or global events introduce instability into work and personal lives.
   Topics: wellbeing, survey-design. Evidence: `L124`, "Measures employee sentiment when societal or global events introduce instability into our work and personal lives".
15. **Developmental 360 feedback** (documentation) - 360 Feedback is listed as Ad Hoc, gathering a manager's strengths and opportunities with regular schedules recommended for roles. Its developmental purpose is distinct from general engagement listening.
   Topics: feedback, growth-development. Evidence: `L130`, "Collects insights into a manager's strengths and opportunities".
16. **Staged prelaunch communications** (documentation) - The suggested sequence begins six weeks before launch with senior-leader introduction and stakeholder planning, then senior-leader survey preparation at five weeks, HRBP introduction/training at four/three weeks, and manager/leader introduction at two weeks. Customize to the program.
   Topics: learning-enablement, glint-admin. Evidence: `L135`, "Customize our email templates and share them during your Viva Glint program design phase. Use these templates and tips to introduce Viva Glint program".
