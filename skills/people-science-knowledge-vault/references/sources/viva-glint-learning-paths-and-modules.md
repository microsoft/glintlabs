## Viva Glint learning paths & modules

`viva-glint-learning-paths-and-modules` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/start/viva-glint-learning-paths-and-modules

Full learning-directory page reviewed. Ten themes route to course offerings; the linked modules, videos, and Masterclasses were not independently reviewed in this directory audit.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Self-paced role-based preparation** (signpost) - The directory recommends learning before a first program and distinguishes manager and administrator routes.
   Topics: learning-enablement, glint-admin. Evidence: `L5`, "self-paced".
2. **Modern engagement methodology** (signpost) - One learning path covers People Success, people-centric measurement, and enabling action.
   Topics: engagement, people-science. Evidence: `L21`, "People Success".
3. **Program design and launch** (signpost) - The setup path covers program design, configuration, launch, and planning a results rollout.
   Topics: survey-design, glint-admin. Evidence: `L31`, "configuration and launch".
4. **Interpretation and ACT conversations** (signpost) - A results path covers report interpretation and sustainable team conversations.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, action-taking. Evidence: `L36`, "ACT conversations".
5. **Vision and strategy alignment** (signpost) - An optional module offers a meeting framework to align stakeholders on business challenges.
   Topics: leadership, employee-listening. Evidence: `L42`, "meeting design".
6. **Executive consultation** (signpost) - An optional course moves from data to insights and executive discussion.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, leadership. Evidence: `L47`, "Executive Consultation".
7. **360 setup and feedback** (signpost) - A separate path covers configuring, launching, and sharing 360 surveys and results.
   Topics: feedback, glint-admin. Evidence: `L52`, "360 survey".
8. **Dashboard and report navigation** (signpost) - Manager learning covers confidentiality, summary, heat map, driver, comment, Focus Area, and conversation features.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, manager-effectiveness. Evidence: `L74`, "confidentiality".
9. **Psychological safety practice with Copilot** (signpost) - Modules progress from recognizing safety to practical and advanced lessons, including Copilot-supported practice.
   Topics: psychological-safety, learning-enablement. Evidence: `L94`, "Copilot".
10. **On-demand listening and safety sessions** (signpost) - The directory links Masterclasses and psychological-safety sessions, including safety amid change.
   Topics: learning-enablement, employee-listening. Evidence: `L60`, "end-to-end journey".
