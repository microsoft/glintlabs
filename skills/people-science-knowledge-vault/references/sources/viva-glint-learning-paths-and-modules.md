## Viva Glint learning paths & modules

`viva-glint-learning-paths-and-modules` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/start/viva-glint-learning-paths-and-modules

Full learning-directory page reviewed. Themes describe role-based training recommendations and route to offerings; linked modules, recordings and Masterclasses were not independently reviewed.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete freshly retrieved public Microsoft Learn article text reviewed, including substantive tables. Sign-in notices and page chrome excluded as evidence. Linked pages, courses, downloads, recordings and image-only details were not reviewed; no refreshed comprehensive figure or media review is claimed.

1. **Self-paced preparation before the first program** (documentation) - The directory recommends its self-paced admin/manager learning before first setup and launch. HR and IT professionals may find both routes useful; this is a recommendation, not a certification prerequisite.
   Topics: learning-enablement, glint-admin. Evidence: `L20`, "Viva Glint learning paths, composed of modules, are carefully created by our Subject Matter Experts to provide a thorough self-paced and self-teachabl".
2. **Core admin methodology and setup routes** (signpost) - Recommended admin learning includes the introduction, modern engagement/People Success measurement and action, and a program-design/setup path spanning dashboard setup, configuration, launch and results rollout. This entry routes to learning; it does not certify completion.
   Topics: learning-enablement, survey-design. Evidence: `L37`, "Get to know Viva Glint's approach to modern engagement   Design a People-Centric Measurement Strategy   Empower Action for Better Results".
3. **Interpretation, navigation and ACT learning** (signpost) - Recommended results learning and manager modules cover confidentiality, dashboards and reports, Focus Areas, Team Conversations and sharing results through sustainable ACT discussions.
   Topics: learning-enablement, survey-interpretation, action-taking. Evidence: `L47`, "Navigate your Viva Glint results Share your Viva Glint results".
4. **Optional vision and executive consultation** (signpost) - Optional modules provide a stakeholder meeting framework for aligning business challenges and guidance for moving from data to insights in Executive Consultation. The directory describes offerings, not their full frameworks.
   Topics: learning-enablement, leadership. Evidence: `L51`, "Provides the meeting design framework to align stakeholders on the core business challenges your organization wants to solve with a Viva Glint survey ".
5. **Separate optional 360 learning path** (signpost) - The optional 360 path addresses configuring, launching and sharing 360 surveys/results; it is distinct from the ordinary recurring-engagement setup route.
   Topics: learning-enablement, feedback. Evidence: `L61`, "Designed to help understand how to configure, launch, and share a 360 survey and results".
6. **Psychological safety practice with Copilot** (signpost) - Manager offerings distinguish recognizing psychological safety from three practical and three advanced lessons, including Copilot-supported practice. These are course descriptions, not evidence that the exercises improve a team's outcomes.
   Topics: learning-enablement, psychological-safety. Evidence: `L85`, "Provides three practical lessons managers can take to cultivate psychological safety on their teams. It also teaches managers how to use Copilot to he".
