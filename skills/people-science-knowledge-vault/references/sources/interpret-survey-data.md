1. **Start with context and employee comments** (recommendation) - Understand organizational and external circumstances, then use comment themes to frame questions before anchoring on a score.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, feedback. Evidence: `s110` at 428.1s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/4021d8c8-2092-4d24-a9e6-601282bd0d0e), "external internal".
2. **Response patterns and listening credibility** (recommendation) - Consider consistency, business context, and visible follow-through when interpreting participation; maximizing response rate can create pressure.
   Topics: employee-listening, methods. Evidence: `s159` at 629.2s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/4021d8c8-2092-4d24-a9e6-601282bd0d0e), "pressured".
3. **Strengths and opportunities are outcome-linked** (documentation) - The demonstrated report prioritizes items highly correlated with engagement; this is prioritization guidance, not proof that changing any one item causes engagement to rise.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, methods. Evidence: `s203` at 757.6s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/4021d8c8-2092-4d24-a9e6-601282bd0d0e), "highly correlated".
4. **Absolute and benchmark views complement each other** (recommendation) - Use both comparisons; some items naturally score lower, and custom items without norms can disappear from the benchmark view.
   Topics: benchmarks, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `s257` at 883.4s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/4021d8c8-2092-4d24-a9e6-601282bd0d0e), "custom items".
5. **Longer trends and persistent low scores** (recommendation) - Look beyond one-cycle movement, recognize progress, and investigate chronic underperformance even when a score barely changes.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, action-taking. Evidence: `s525` at 1809.8s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/4021d8c8-2092-4d24-a9e6-601282bd0d0e), "underperforming".
6. **Practical versus statistical differences** (method) - Interpret score gaps using group size, repeated patterns, and business meaning rather than treating a significance test as the whole decision.
   Topics: methods, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `s345` at 1217.0s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/4021d8c8-2092-4d24-a9e6-601282bd0d0e), "meaningful in practical terms".
7. **Prioritize by agency and time horizon** (recommendation) - Select actions aligned to business priorities, feasible at the responsible organizational level, and balanced between short- and longer-term work.
   Topics: action-taking, leadership. Evidence: `s494` at 1669.4s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/4021d8c8-2092-4d24-a9e6-601282bd0d0e), "equally actionable".
8. **Question-led heatmap exploration** (recommendation) - Start with meaningful research questions, compare scores and changes, and order sequential attributes to avoid aimless subgroup searching.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, inclusion. Evidence: `s629` at 2134.9s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/4021d8c8-2092-4d24-a9e6-601282bd0d0e), "research questions".
9. **Small groups require extra due diligence** (method) - Do not interpret a small team's score as equally stable as a large group's; use trends and privacy-safe qualitative context without treating sample-size rules of thumb as guarantees.
   Topics: methods, survey-interpretation, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `s786` at 2657.9s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/4021d8c8-2092-4d24-a9e6-601282bd0d0e), "due diligence".
10. **Constructive, privacy-respecting comment review** (recommendation) - Use comments to understand experiences and possible actions without taking criticism personally or trying to identify who wrote it. Filtered comment subsets are not automatically representative.
   Topics: feedback, psychological-safety, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `s925` at 3302.4s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/4021d8c8-2092-4d24-a9e6-601282bd0d0e), "Do not try to identify".
