## Research Drop: Empowering Managers for an AI-First Future

`research-drop-4468191` | research-drop | reviewed

**Priority:** New catalog; before relevant original-vault resources.

**Source:** https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftvivablog/research-drop-empowering-managers-for-an-ai-first-future/4468191

November 2025 public article, measurement-item appendix, and four public figures reviewed; no internal deck appendix used. July 2025 panel n=1,800, including 520 managers. Comparisons reflect concurrent self-reports, not effects produced by a manager training intervention.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Also reviewed OCR of 4 public figures; numeric claims cross-checked against written text where available. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

**Study-family links:** agentic-teaming-trust-july-2025

1. **Managers bridge strategy and execution** (framework) - The article argues for an active, redesigned manager role in human-agent transformation.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, ai-strategy. Evidence: `L11`, "bridge".
2. **Optimism does not equal support** (finding) - Managers are broadly positive about agents but many feel overwhelmed, strained, or insufficiently supported.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, wellbeing. Evidence: `L28`, "56%".
3. **Curiosity and systems orchestration** (framework) - The proposed role moves from control and delegation toward curiosity, systems understanding, role modeling, and orchestration.
   Topics: leadership, work-design. Evidence: `L31`, "systems intelligence".
4. **Manager-specific enablement gaps** (finding) - Tools, opportunities, and AI training are not reaching all managers; attendance and codified guidance remain limited.
   Topics: ai-skills, manager-effectiveness. Evidence: `L36`, "62%".
5. **Transfer practical workflow knowledge** (recommendation) - Managers can identify suitable use cases, create prompt guidance, and coach colleagues in repeatable practices.
   Topics: ai-skills, learning-enablement. Evidence: `L34`, "unique visibility".
6. **Lead learning through visible experiments** (recommendation) - Sharing small trials, success stories, and personal learning makes the change tangible.
   Topics: leadership, learning-enablement. Evidence: `L52`, "trial projects".
7. **Role modeling and value, judgment, trust** (finding) - Employees reporting effective leader role modeling also report higher agent value, critical thinking, and trust.
   Topics: ai-value, ai-governance. Evidence: `L54`, "role model".
8. **Psychological safety and agent readiness** (finding) - Psychologically safe respondents report more frequent agent use and stronger readiness and value.
   Topics: psychological-safety, ai-adoption. Evidence: `L62`, "1.4x".
9. **Team norms and routine feedback** (recommendation) - Clarify human-AI roles and make questions and experience-sharing routine rather than leaving feedback to chance.
   Topics: feedback, ai-governance. Evidence: `L65`, "41%".
10. **Different measures for different outcomes** (method) - The published item appendix distinguishes critical thinking, trust, individual/team readiness, RIVA, and team value.
   Topics: methods, ai-value. Evidence: `L70`, "composite scales".
