## What’s New with Viva Glint Benchmarks?

`what-s-new-with-viva-glint-benchmarks` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `top5`.

**Source:** https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/viva_glint_blog/what%E2%80%99s-new-with-viva-glint-benchmarks/4448380

2025 benchmark release and methodology update. Preserve release dates, refresh windows, opt-in rules, and the distinction between explanatory hypotheses and calculation changes.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete current cached public article body reviewed; comments, page chrome, and link directories excluded from substantive evidence. Embedded figures were not re-reviewed; claims are restricted to readable prose/page text, not image-only details. Linked publications, courses, and other companion assets are not newly reviewed unless explicitly stated.

1. **Customer-only norms require standard items** (documentation) - The 2025 release describes customer-only benchmarks for standard Viva Glint items, drawing on a broad customer database; custom or altered questions are not automatically benchmarkable.
   Topics: benchmarks, survey-design. Evidence: `L2`, "only generated for Viva Glint standard items".
2. **Suites answer different comparison questions** (documentation) - Global, industry, country/region, lifecycle, and specialized suites supply different reference populations; onboarding and exit items appear where enough data exists. Choose the reference population relevant to the question rather than treating suites as interchangeable.
   Topics: benchmarks, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L4`, "different contexts".
3. **High-performing norms are item-specific thresholds** (documentation) - Top-10% and top-25% benchmarks are the threshold scores needed to enter those tiers for a given item, not the average scores of the highest-scoring organizations.
   Topics: benchmarks, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L6`, "threshold score".
4. **Opt-in links external comparison access and contribution** (documentation) - As described for M365 Viva in 2025, organizations opt in to anonymous benchmark processing to access external norms. Opted-out customers are excluded and see internal comparisons; administrators can change the choice.
   Topics: benchmarks, privacy-confidentiality, glint-admin. Evidence: `L12`, "organizations must opt in".
5. **Eyes-off generation protects customer data** (documentation) - The automated process produces aggregated benchmark scores without People Science directly viewing customer data. The release explicitly states that switching to eyes-off processing did not change calculations.
   Topics: benchmarks, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L17`, "without accessing or seeing customer data directly".
6. **Refresh cadence differs by suite** (documentation) - The release describes twice-yearly global and high-performing norms and annual industry/country norms using a rolling 12-month window; some specialty suites may need 24 months for coverage.
   Topics: benchmarks, methods. Evidence: `L21`, "refreshed twice a year".
7. **Coverage depends on item use** (documentation) - Only items meeting strict usage thresholds receive benchmarks; the release reports over 175 benchmarked items but supplies no universal item-level minimum in this article.
   Topics: benchmarks, survey-design, methods. Evidence: `L28`, "strict usage threshold".
8. **Check the comparator and its vintage in reports** (documentation) - Admins select a default internal or external comparison, while authorized report users can change it. Suite name/year, detailed question values, heatmaps, and exports help distinguish reference scores from team scores.
   Topics: benchmarks, survey-interpretation, glint-admin. Evidence: `L36`, "identified by name/year".
9. **Small 2025 score movements are descriptive** (finding) - Compared with the prior 2024 run, the release says about half of items were unchanged and half changed by one point; this does not establish a general market trend or an effect of the new processing system.
   Topics: benchmarks, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L41`, "about half the items had no change".
10. **Consent and item mix can shift benchmark composition** (hypothesis) - Customer opt-outs and changes in item utilization can alter which organizations contribute each item's norm. These are offered as possible explanations for shifts, not tested causal decompositions.
   Topics: benchmarks, methods. Evidence: `L43`, "changing the mix of customers".
11. **Migration pause broke the usual overlapping windows** (documentation) - The December 2024 refresh pause means July 2024–June 2025 data were compared with April 2023–March 2024, not the customary six-month-overlapping annual windows. Interpret that release comparison accordingly.
   Topics: benchmarks, methods, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L44`, "paused the regular refresh in December 2024".
