## What’s New with Viva Glint Benchmarks?

1. **Customer-only standard-item norms** (documentation) - Benchmarks use customer responses to standard Glint items, drawing on a broad cross-company database.
   Topics: benchmarks, survey-design. Evidence: `L13`, "standard items".
2. **Benchmark suites and percentile thresholds** (documentation) - Global, industry, region, lifecycle, and specialized suites answer different comparison needs; high-performing norms are item-level thresholds.
   Topics: benchmarks. Evidence: `L22`, "threshold score".
3. **Consent and benchmark access** (documentation) - Organizations must opt in to anonymous benchmark processing before external comparisons become available.
   Topics: benchmarks, glint-admin. Evidence: `L35`, "must opt in".
4. **Eyes-off confidential generation** (documentation) - The update describes automated aggregate processing without People Scientists directly viewing customer data.
   Topics: benchmarks, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L44`, "without accessing".
5. **Rolling data and refresh cadence** (documentation) - Global and high-performing suites refresh semiannually, while industry and country suites refresh annually using recent data.
   Topics: benchmarks, methods. Evidence: `L55`, "twice yearly".
6. **Item usage and coverage thresholds** (documentation) - Only sufficiently used items are benchmarked; some specialty suites need a longer window.
   Topics: benchmarks, methods. Evidence: `L70`, "strict usage threshold".
7. **Comparator selection in reports** (documentation) - Admins select defaults and authorized users can switch comparison groups in reports.
   Topics: benchmarks, glint-admin. Evidence: `L83`, "change the external".
8. **Heatmaps and exported comparisons** (documentation) - Benchmark comparisons are available in reporting views and exports, not just a separate norms document.
   Topics: benchmarks, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L91`, "Excel exports".
9. **Small changes in the 2025 refresh** (finding) - The release reports most items unchanged or moving by one point relative to the prior release.
   Topics: benchmarks, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L99`, "about half".
10. **Composition and window caveats** (method) - Opt-out, non-overlapping refresh windows, and item-utilization changes are possible explanations for shifts; eyes-off processing did not change the calculation.
   Topics: benchmarks, methods. Evidence: `L104`, "paused the regular refresh".
