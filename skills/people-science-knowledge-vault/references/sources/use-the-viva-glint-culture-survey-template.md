## Use the Viva Glint Culture Survey template

`use-the-viva-glint-culture-survey-template` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `medium`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/culture-template

Complete Culture Survey guide, including item tables. Broad business-benefit statistics have limited methodological context on this page and are not used as causal evidence. Source item-table anomalies should be verified before copying exact wording.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Culture as observable group behavior** (method) - Measure recurring group behavior and norms rather than treating culture as an individual's feelings.
   Topics: survey-design, work-design. Evidence: `L5`, "group phenomenon".
2. **Intended versus experienced culture** (framework) - The program diagnoses whether lived culture matches leadership intentions and supports future success.
   Topics: leadership, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L7`, "intended culture".
3. **Transformation and integration baseline** (documentation) - Culture measurement supports transformation, leadership transitions, and merger or acquisition integration.
   Topics: change-management, employee-listening. Evidence: `L9`, "merger or acquisition".
4. **Whole-workforce inclusion** (recommendation) - The guide recommends involving all employees to improve insight and support for cultural transformation.
   Topics: inclusion, employee-listening. Evidence: `L11`, "Include all employees".
5. **Eight cultural dimensions** (documentation) - Items cover collaboration, purpose, humanity, alignment, integrity, execution, learning, and adaptability.
   Topics: survey-design, work-design, meaningful-work. Evidence: `L18`, "eight key outcomes".
6. **Speak-up and constructive collaboration** (documentation) - The item set includes cross-group coordination and speaking openly without fear of consequences.
   Topics: psychological-safety, work-design, connection. Evidence: `L39`, "without fear".
7. **Belonging, wellbeing, and fair decisions** (documentation) - Culture items operationalize mutual care, belonging, transparency, and objective decision-making.
   Topics: inclusion, wellbeing, connection. Evidence: `L66`, "fairly and objectively".
8. **Learning and adaptive execution** (documentation) - Questions cover learning from mistakes, seeking feedback, innovation, timely decisions, and reliably delivering commitments.
   Topics: feedback, learning-enablement. Evidence: `L57`, "learn from mistakes".
9. **Open text and comparison context** (documentation) - The program combines strengths/improvement prompts, comment analytics, and culture benchmarks; optional items address other sentiments.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, benchmarks. Evidence: `L21`, "open-ended feedback".
10. **Separate measurement and reinforce culture** (recommendation) - Do not casually combine full culture and engagement programs; reinforce desired behavior across the lifecycle and revisit experiences regularly.
   Topics: survey-design, change-management. Evidence: `L143`, "isn't recommended".
