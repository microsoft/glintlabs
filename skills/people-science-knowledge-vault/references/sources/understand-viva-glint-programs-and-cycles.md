## Understand Viva Glint programs and cycles

`understand-viva-glint-programs-and-cycles` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/program-cycle-edits

Full recurring-program/cycle editing documentation. Unlinking changes configuration inheritance, not historical reporting, and relinking is not currently supported.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete freshly retrieved public Microsoft Learn article text reviewed, including substantive tables. Sign-in notices and page chrome excluded as evidence. Linked pages, courses, downloads, recordings and image-only details were not reviewed; no refreshed comprehensive figure or media review is claimed.

1. **Programs contain timed cycles** (documentation) - A program holds shared configuration such as languages, confidentiality and distribution. A cycle is one survey occasion, normally named with its launch month/year; recurring cycles allow score trends.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L21`, "Survey program : A container that houses survey configuration settings (like available languages, confidentiality thresholds, or distribution) for its".
2. **Linked cycles inherit program changes** (documentation) - By default cycles are linked and receive program-level changes. Edit the overall program for future repeated surveys to avoid unnecessary individual adjustments; untouched cycles repeat program emails, text and questions.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L23`, "Linked : By default, all surveys cycles are linked to the overall survey program, meaning that any changes made at the program level automatically app".
3. **Local edits permanently stop inheritance** (documentation) - Cycle edits apply only to that survey, leave future surveys unchanged and unlink the edited cycle, preventing later program updates from flowing into it. Relinking is not currently supported.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L29`, "Edits made at the cycle level  unlink  the cycle from the overall survey program and program updates no longer flow to the unlinked, edited survey cyc".
4. **Reporting and trend continuity** (documentation) - Unlinking affects survey settings, not reporting or trend data; it should not be mistaken for loss of historical results.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, glint-admin. Evidence: `L39`, "Unlinked survey cycles only impact survey settings and don't affect reporting.".
5. **Approved-cycle editing workflow** (documentation) - For an Approved survey, select the upcoming/live cycle, accept the unlink warning, and save local Distribution, Questions or Communications changes. The cycle then displays Unlinked from Program.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L34`, "In the  Upcoming and Live  section, select the survey cycle to edit. An  Edit Survey  dialog appears letting you know that any edits unlink the survey".
6. **Live reminder changes require cycle edits** (documentation) - Changing reminder send dates or adding reminders to a live survey requires cycle-level edits and their unlinking consequence. Other live changes require the separate live-survey guidance.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L41`, "If a survey is live, and you want to adjust survey reminder send dates or add reminders, you need to  make edits at the cycle level . For other change".
