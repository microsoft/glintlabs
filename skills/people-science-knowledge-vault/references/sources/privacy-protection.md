## Viva Pulse privacy and data protection

`privacy-protection` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/pulse/get-started/privacy-and-data-protection

Complete short Pulse privacy page. It does not promise absolute anonymity: free text can reveal identities and privileged access may identify responses in certain circumstances.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete freshly retrieved public Microsoft Learn article text reviewed, including substantive tables. Sign-in notices and page chrome excluded as evidence. Linked pages, courses, downloads, recordings and image-only details were not reviewed; no refreshed comprehensive figure or media review is claimed.

1. **Recipient minimum gates sending** (documentation) - A minimum recipient threshold must be met before an author can send a Pulse. Pulse admins may update the threshold for organizational needs; this page supplies no universal numeric default.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L20`, "Due to the potential sensitivity about how the individual employee’s feedback could be used, a minimum threshold for recipients must be met for the au".
2. **Response minimum independently gates results** (documentation) - A separate minimum response count is required before feedback results can be reviewed after collection ends. Admins can update this threshold; meeting the recipient count is not equivalent to obtaining enough responses.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L20`, "Due to the potential sensitivity about how the individual employee’s feedback could be used, a minimum threshold for recipients must be met for the au".
3. **Authors see results only after closure** (documentation) - Pulse withholds results from the author until the request has closed, in addition to the response-count protection.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L21`, "Additionally, Viva Pulse leverages research-backed surveys developed by People Scientists with a focus on maximizing the effectiveness of the response".
4. **Write-in text can identify responders** (documentation) - Authors do not see names or other identifiers unless employees choose to include them in written responses. This protection is not a promise that free text cannot reveal identity.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L21`, "Additionally, Viva Pulse leverages research-backed surveys developed by People Scientists with a focus on maximizing the effectiveness of the response".
5. **Privileged-access exception to anonymity** (documentation) - Administrators and privileged users with elevated permissions may be able to identify employees and responses in certain circumstances. The page does not enumerate those circumstances or promise absolute anonymity.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L22`, "Only administrators and privileged users with elevated permissions may be able to identify employees and their responses in certain circumstances.".
6. **Aggregate sharing and optional open text** (documentation) - After closure, authors may share reports showing aggregate information and may choose to share open-text responses with team members. Organization-configured connected experiences can also share results; do not assume all sharing excludes text or happens only through the author.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L24`, "Viva Pulse provides authors the option to share reports once their Pulse closes. If results are shared, only the aggregate information is viewable by ".
