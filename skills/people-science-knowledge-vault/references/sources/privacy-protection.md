## Viva Pulse privacy and data protection

`privacy-protection` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/pulse/get-started/privacy-and-data-protection

Complete short Pulse privacy page. It does not promise absolute anonymity: free text can reveal identities and privileged access may identify responses in certain circumstances.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Sensitivity of employee feedback** (framework) - The privacy model recognizes that employees may worry about how individual feedback is used.
   Topics: privacy-confidentiality, employee-listening. Evidence: `L3`, "potential sensitivity".
2. **Minimum recipient count** (documentation) - An author must meet a recipient threshold before sending a Pulse.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L3`, "minimum threshold for recipients".
3. **Minimum response count** (documentation) - A separate response threshold controls whether team results are released.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L3`, "minimum number of responses".
4. **Organization-controlled thresholds** (documentation) - Pulse administrators can update threshold values for organizational needs.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L3`, "update these threshold values".
5. **Survey design as responder protection** (documentation) - The page describes People Science survey design as supporting effective responses and provider protection.
   Topics: people-science, survey-design. Evidence: `L4`, "protecting the feedback providers".
6. **Results withheld until closure** (documentation) - Authors do not see results while a Pulse is still collecting feedback.
   Topics: privacy-confidentiality, pulse-admin. Evidence: `L4`, "after the Pulse has closed".
7. **Free-text identification risk** (documentation) - Names and identifiers are not shown to authors unless respondents put identifying information into written feedback.
   Topics: privacy-confidentiality, feedback. Evidence: `L4`, "write-in response".
8. **Privileged-access exception** (documentation) - Administrators or elevated users may be able to identify employees and responses under some circumstances.
   Topics: privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L5`, "certain circumstances".
9. **Controlled sharing of reports and comments** (documentation) - Shared reports contain aggregate information, with optional text sharing and possible organization-connected experiences.
   Topics: privacy-confidentiality, pulse-admin. Evidence: `L7`, "open text responses".
10. **Privacy notice and compliance references** (signpost) - The article links the end-user notice and privacy statement for further protection and GDPR questions.
   Topics: privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L9`, "GDPR".
