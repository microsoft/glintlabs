## Harness Viva Glint and Viva Pulse to drive continuous improvements

`continuous-improvement` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `high`.

**Source:** https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/viva_glint_blog/harness-the-power-of-viva-glint-and-viva-pulse-to-drive-continuous-improvements/4428922

Product integration announcement with released features, preview observations, future plans, and license requirements. Roadmap statements are not evidence of present availability.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete current cached public article body reviewed; comments, page chrome, and link directories excluded from substantive evidence. Embedded figures were not re-reviewed; claims are restricted to readable prose/page text, not image-only details. Linked publications, courses, and other companion assets are not newly reviewed unless explicitly stated.

1. **Use local pulses to check progress between engagement cycles** (recommendation) - The announcement recommends timely manager follow-up on Glint strengths and opportunities, gathering more context, checking progress, and adjusting action before the next organization-wide survey.
   Topics: action-taking, employee-listening. Evidence: `L1`, "make mid-point adjustments".
2. **Driver-linked questions standardize follow-up** (documentation) - The announced Glint follow-up template selects Pulse library questions from the drivers chosen by the author; this is a topic-based starting point rather than evidence that every team should use identical follow-up content.
   Topics: survey-design, pulse-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L3`, "automatically selected based on Glint drivers".
3. **Reminders and recipient prefilling reduce setup friction** (documentation) - The announcement describes a default six-week follow-up email and recipient prefilling from the Glint program. Timing is configurable, so six weeks is not a universal required cadence.
   Topics: pulse-admin, glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L4`, "track team progress to date".
4. **Glint admins control program eligibility and outreach** (documentation) - Glint administrators choose enabled programs, notification roles, timing, and email content. This organization-level control differs from Pulse template-content administration.
   Topics: glint-admin, pulse-admin. Evidence: `L6`, "configure which Glint survey programs".
5. **Pulse admins control follow-up content** (documentation) - Pulse content administrators can edit, remove, or deactivate content in the Glint follow-up template and question topic.
   Topics: pulse-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L7`, "editing, removing, or deactivating content".
6. **Email and report banners provide entry points** (documentation) - Managers can begin a Pulse follow-up through the notification or a banner in Glint team/executive summaries; the announcement distinguishes these entry points from planned inline authoring.
   Topics: glint-admin, pulse-admin. Evidence: `L8`, "team summary and executive summary".
7. **Confidential feedback can deepen team conversations** (documentation) - In-app Pulse support is described as allowing managers to ask for further feedback on specific questions; this is a listening feature, not permission to identify respondents.
   Topics: feedback, privacy-confidentiality, action-taking. Evidence: `L9`, "Confidential team conversations".
8. **Copilot summaries combine scores and comments** (documentation) - The announced Pulse report summary draws on team question scores and open-ended responses. It summarizes available feedback rather than independently verifying causes of the team's results.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, pulse-admin. Evidence: `L10`, "question scores and open-ended responses".
9. **Early adoption signals are limited preview observations** (case-study) - Microsoft reported sevenfold more Pulse follow-ups in February than January 2025 and a 7.5% email clickthrough; nine customers joined private preview. Feedback suggested a useful gap-filler, but these observations do not prove better engagement caused by follow-ups.
   Topics: methods, action-taking. Evidence: `L13`, "7x increase".
10. **Roadmap items are not released capabilities** (documentation) - Inline Glint authoring, personalized Copilot authoring, admin telemetry, automatic delegate access, and viewing Pulse data in Glint are listed as future work, not established current functionality.
   Topics: glint-admin, pulse-admin. Evidence: `L18`, "As we continue to iterate on this integration".
11. **Licensing gates the described integration** (documentation) - The announcement lists Viva Suite or Viva Workplace Analytics and Employee Feedback as qualifying subscriptions; verify present-day official setup and licensing documentation before implementation.
   Topics: glint-admin, pulse-admin. Evidence: `L25`, "one of the following subscription licenses".
