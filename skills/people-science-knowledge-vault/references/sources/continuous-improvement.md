1. **Mid-cycle follow-up** (documentation) - Pulse follows up on Glint strengths and opportunities so managers can assess progress and adjust between engagement surveys.
   Topics: employee-listening, action-taking. Evidence: `L1`, "mid-point adjustments".

   Topics: glint-admin, pulse-admin. Evidence: `L6`, "6 weeks".

   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L10`, "configure which Glint".
5. **Pulse content administration** (documentation) - Pulse content admins can edit, remove, or deactivate follow-up content.
   Topics: pulse-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L12`, "deactivating content".
6. **In-product follow-up entry points** (documentation) - Managers can start follow-ups through report banners as well as notification emails.
   Topics: action-taking, glint-admin. Evidence: `L14`, "team summary".
7. **Confidential team dialogue** (documentation) - Pulse provides support for requesting additional team feedback on particular questions.
   Topics: feedback, psychological-safety. Evidence: `L16`, "more feedback".
8. **Copilot report summaries** (documentation) - A summary combines question scores with open-ended responses at the top of the Pulse report.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, ai-agents. Evidence: `L18`, "open-ended responses".
9. **Early adoption signals** (case-study) - Microsoft usage and nine preview customers provide early feedback, not causal proof that the integration raises engagement.
   Topics: methods, action-taking. Evidence: `L22`, "9 customers".
10. **Roadmap and licensing boundaries** (documentation) - Inline authoring, further Copilot support, telemetry, delegation, and Glint-side Pulse analysis are listed as future work; usage requires an eligible subscription.
   Topics: glint-admin, pulse-admin. Evidence: `L26`, "working".
