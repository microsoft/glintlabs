## Redefining High Performance in the New Era of Work

`redefining-high-performance` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `curated`.

**Source:** https://cdn-dynmedia-1.microsoft.com/is/content/microsoftcorp/microsoft/final/en-us/microsoft-brand/documents/Microsoft-HPO-Guide-Oct-2023.pdf

All 28 pages of the exact October 2023 guide recovered from Microsoft's official CDN. Main survey: 1,100 leaders, September 2023, nine markets, organizations with 1,000+ employees. It also cites separate engagement/market and learning analyses. Leader perceptions and customer examples are not causal intervention evidence. HPO definition here selects 15%, unlike 17% in the later AI-readiness study.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete extracted text of the public 28-page PDF. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

**Study-family links:** high-performance-september-2023

1. **Performance beyond financial outcomes** (framework) - Define success through a context-appropriate combination of financial results, innovation, collaboration, growth, and resilience.
   Topics: leadership, work-design. Evidence: `p14`, "nuanced approach".
2. **Three mutually reinforcing pillars** (framework) - The framework integrates engaged employees, productive teams, and resilient business rather than optimizing individual output alone.
   Topics: engagement, work-design. Evidence: `p7`, "three pillars".
3. **Manager capability is important and difficult** (finding) - Leaders rate managers' ability to empower and develop teams as both critical and particularly difficult to master.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, leadership, empowerment. Evidence: `p9`, "most challenging".
4. **Team productivity needs enabling conditions** (framework) - Collaboration requires efficient processes, useful data, and tools, not just expectations of greater individual effort.
   Topics: work-design. Evidence: `p10`, "efficient processes".
5. **Resilience through skills and innovation** (finding) - Agility, upskilling, and experimentation help define resilience; learning opportunities also correlate with favorable employee outcomes.
   Topics: learning-enablement, change-management, growth-development. Evidence: `p11`, "27 times".
6. **Prioritize by importance and difficulty** (framework) - The guide separates transformational investments, amplification of promising practices, and optimization of foundational capabilities.
   Topics: leadership, action-taking. Evidence: `p17`, "Amplification".
7. **AI as a proposed performance accelerant** (finding) - Leaders see AI potential in tedious work, inefficient work culture, burnout, manager overload, and skill gaps; these are anticipated benefits.
   Topics: ai-strategy, ai-value. Evidence: `p19`, "potential to address".
8. **Deep collaboration includes disagreement** (finding) - Co-investment, sponsoring others, and constructive debate are less common than friendly collaboration and more frequent among HPO respondents.
   Topics: leadership, work-design, connection. Evidence: `p22`, "politely debating".
9. **HR-IT partnership and bridging roles** (finding) - Technology investment decisions are often siloed; digital-transformation roles can translate between human needs and technical delivery.
   Topics: hr-it, change-management. Evidence: `p23`, "mediator and translator".
10. **Assess, act, and measure in context** (recommendation) - Combine active and passive signals, choose actions, and assess progress; PepsiCo illustrates meeting and shared-accountability changes, not a controlled trial.
   Topics: employee-listening, action-taking. Evidence: `p26`, "Measure Impact".
