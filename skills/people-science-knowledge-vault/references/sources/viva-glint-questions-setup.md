## Viva Glint Questions setup

`viva-glint-questions-setup` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/questions-setup

Complete Engagement-program question configuration documentation; the page explicitly excludes 360 programs. Product rules and eNPS arithmetic are not employee-outcome research.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Participant messages and branding** (documentation) - Customize introductions and thank-you text, macros, logos, and supported links; review every program language.
   Topics: glint-admin, inclusion. Evidence: `L37`, "can't be added".
2. **Cycle-specific item allocation** (documentation) - Blank recurring programs can have cycles added, and each cycle must include at least one selected question.
   Topics: survey-design, glint-admin. Evidence: `L179`, "at least one question".
3. **Library-first question selection** (recommendation) - Search and filter existing library items before creating custom questions, accounting for company survey policies.
   Topics: survey-design, methods. Evidence: `L197`, "search the Question Library".
4. **Custom-item tradeoffs** (documentation) - New custom items have no preloaded translations or external benchmarks and are not automatically included in standard risk indices or action templates.
   Topics: survey-design, benchmarks. Evidence: `L184`, "no preloaded translations".
5. **eNPS calculation versus storage scale** (documentation) - eNPS subtracts detractor percentage from promoter percentage; the respondent-facing 0-10 scale is internally stored on 1-11 without changing reporting.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, methods. Evidence: `L229`, "1–11 scale".
6. **Safe edits and response-option history** (documentation) - Check associated programs before item edits; add new choice values and hide old ones rather than overwriting labels and corrupting trends.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L310`, "trend issues".
7. **Editable fields and response controls** (documentation) - The page distinguishes immutable IDs/types/scales from editable wording, labels, comments, optionality, benchmarks, and action-template mappings.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L312`, "view only".
8. **Sections and section breaks** (documentation) - A break is a transient message; a survey section keeps a header associated with its questions.
   Topics: survey-design, glint-admin. Evidence: `L404`, "remains at the top".
9. **Targeting versus display logic** (documentation) - Targeting depends on roles or distribution lists, whereas display logic responds to prior survey answers.
   Topics: survey-design, glint-admin. Evidence: `L471`, "responses to previous questions".
10. **Deletion and launch boundaries** (documentation) - Removing a program item does not delete it from the library; the page also warns that live surveys restrict editing.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L484`, "still exists".
