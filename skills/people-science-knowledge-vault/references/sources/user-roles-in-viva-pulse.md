1. **Users can hold multiple Pulse roles** (documentation) - Feedback author, feedback provider and Viva Pulse admin are not mutually exclusive; a user may hold more than one.
   Topics: pulse-admin. Evidence: `L19`, "Viva Pulse users can do different tasks based on their roles. Users can have one or more of the following roles:".
2. **Author audiences and optional self-inclusion** (documentation) - Authors can send template-based requests to direct reports, teammates or other organizational users and optionally include themselves as recipients.
   Topics: pulse-admin, feedback. Evidence: `L24`, "Feedback authors are users in your organization who can send Pulse requests to their direct reports, team members, or other users in the organization.".
3. **Adding questions requires organizational enablement** (documentation) - Authors may add their own questions to templates only if their organization enables adding new questions.
   Topics: pulse-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L25`, "If adding new questions is enabled for your organization, authors can add their own questions to any of the templates. For more information, see  Set ".
4. **Editing stock questions requires a separate setting** (documentation) - Authors can update, reorder or delete provided questions only when editing stock questions is enabled; this is distinct from permission to add questions.
   Topics: pulse-admin, survey-design. Evidence: `L26`, "If editing stock questions is enabled for your organization, authors can update, reorder, or delete the provided questions in any of the templates. Fo".
5. **Unsent work is autosaved** (documentation) - Pulse request work is saved automatically as authors make changes, allowing them to return to an unfinished request later.
   Topics: pulse-admin. Evidence: `L27`, "Work in progress is automatically saved as updates are made, so if an author isn't ready to send their Pulse yet, they can come back to it later.".
6. **Results require closure and enough responses** (documentation) - Authors view request results only once the Pulse closes and meets the minimum response count. They can share the report to facilitate team conversations.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality, feedback. Evidence: `L28`, "Authors can view the feedback results of their Pulse request once it closes and meets the minimum number of responses required. Authors can also share".
7. **Sending requires a qualifying Pulse license** (documentation) - An author must hold a valid license including Pulse to send requests. The page lists Viva Suite, Viva Workplace Analytics and Employee Feedback, Viva Glint and the Admin-led Trial as current examples and directs readers to the current licensing list.
   Topics: pulse-admin. Evidence: `L29`, "To send a Pulse request, a user must have a valid license that includes Viva Pulse. Qualifying licenses currently include Viva Suite, Viva Workplace A".
8. **Providers belong to the same Entra tenant** (documentation) - Feedback providers receive requests as members of the organization's Microsoft Entra tenant and supply the responses used for aggregate reporting. Do not infer cross-tenant recipient support.
   Topics: pulse-admin, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `L31`, "Feedback providers are the users in an organization who receive a Pulse request. Pulse requests can be received by users if they are part of your orga".
9. **Admin configuration and authorized assignment** (documentation) - Pulse admins configure the end-user experience in Pulse. An appropriately authorized administrator, such as a Privileged Role Administrator or Global Administrator, assigns the role in the Entra admin center.
   Topics: pulse-admin. Evidence: `L33`, "Users with the Viva Pulse admin role can configure the Viva Pulse experience for their end users from within the Viva Pulse admin experience. Viva Pul".
10. **Admin licensing is a separate requirement** (documentation) - The page separately lists Pulse Standalone, Viva Glint, Viva Insights Bundle, Viva Suite or the Admin-led trial for admins. Do not assume the author examples and admin list are identical or permanently exhaustive; verify current licensing guidance.
   Topics: pulse-admin. Evidence: `L33`, "Users with the Viva Pulse admin role can configure the Viva Pulse experience for their end users from within the Viva Pulse admin experience. Viva Pul".
