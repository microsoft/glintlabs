## Think like a People Scientist: Leading AI Change with Employee Sentiment

`think-like-a-people-scientist-leading-ai-change-with-employee-sentiment` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `top5`.

**Source:** https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftvivablog/think-like-a-people-scientist-leading-ai-change-with-employee-sentiment/4412911

May 2025 public recap plus the complete 45-minute public webinar audio, locally machine-transcribed and reviewed. Practitioner recommendations, customer anecdotes, and secondary presentations of research are distinct from new empirical studies. Access/use comparisons can be confounded; perceived productivity is not financial ROI. Historic item, benchmark, and integration statements require current product verification.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. Complete local machine transcript of embedded public webinar audio reviewed; visual-only content and untranscribed demos not certified.

**Study-family links:** ai-readiness-april-2024

**Reviewed webinar audio:** https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/ebe58201-624e-4975-a2df-de2dd1ee154b (publisher-reviewed machine transcript; not hosted here; visual-only content not certified).

1. **The human learning curve of AI change** (case-study) - A presenter and an anonymized customer example describe uncertainty and returning to familiar work under pressure; these anecdotes suggest listening for learning friction rather than assuming low use means disinterest.
   Topics: change-management, ai-adoption. Evidence: `s224` at 835.4s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/ebe58201-624e-4975-a2df-de2dd1ee154b), "regress back".
2. **Baseline and continuing employee dialogue** (recommendation) - Establish a sentiment starting point and keep measuring and discussing experiences as AI capabilities and implementation evolve.
   Topics: employee-listening, ai-adoption. Evidence: `s436` at 1681.0s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/ebe58201-624e-4975-a2df-de2dd1ee154b), "establishing a baseline".
3. **Business outcomes, sentiment, and deeper inquiry** (recommendation) - Combine business and employee-experience evidence with usage information, focus groups, and interviews; no single source supplies the full implementation story.
   Topics: ai-value, employee-listening. Evidence: `s405` at 1584.6s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/ebe58201-624e-4975-a2df-de2dd1ee154b), "focus groups and interviews".
4. **Frontline perspectives expose leadership blind spots** (recommendation) - Include people close to the work and compare leadership assumptions with employee views of communication and involvement. The research examples are secondary reports, not a new independent study.
   Topics: inclusion, leadership. Evidence: `s445` at 1709.4s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/ebe58201-624e-4975-a2df-de2dd1ee154b), "blind spots".
5. **Shared HR and IT measurement ownership** (recommendation) - Build collaborative working groups, shared KPIs, and explicit data-management and privacy discussions rather than treating AI measurement as an IT-only task.
   Topics: hr-it, ai-governance, privacy-confidentiality. Evidence: `s603` at 2211.5s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/ebe58201-624e-4975-a2df-de2dd1ee154b), "shared KPIs".
6. **Match readiness and impact questions to rollout stage** (recommendation) - Use readiness questions before or early in rollout and impact questions as experience accumulates; leadership and cultural conditions can be measured before broad AI access.
   Topics: survey-design, ai-adoption. Evidence: `s503` at 1886.9s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/ebe58201-624e-4975-a2df-de2dd1ee154b), "readiness and impact questions".
7. **Lightweight listening within an existing survey** (recommendation) - When survey burden is a barrier, add a focused AI comment question to an existing listening program rather than automatically launching another full survey.
   Topics: survey-design, employee-listening. Evidence: `s512` at 1918.0s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/ebe58201-624e-4975-a2df-de2dd1ee154b), "open-ended comment".
8. **Perceived productivity and practical use cases** (recommendation) - Ask both whether AI feels helpful and how it is used, then organize concrete examples by function or job family. Self-reported productivity is useful experience evidence, not a financial ROI calculation.
   Topics: ai-value, work-design. Evidence: `s671` at 2484.7s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/ebe58201-624e-4975-a2df-de2dd1ee154b), "specifically are using this".
9. **Attributes reveal adoption differences without proving effects** (method) - Compare appropriate user/non-user or organizational groups to explore resource, engagement, and experience patterns; selection and other confounders prevent causal attribution.
   Topics: methods, ai-adoption. Evidence: `s536` at 2000.4s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/ebe58201-624e-4975-a2df-de2dd1ee154b), "user versus non-user".
10. **Manager pulses complement organizational listening** (recommendation) - Use brief local feedback during rollout to understand goals and value, while considering when leadership also needs a broader organization-level picture.
   Topics: pulse-admin, manager-effectiveness. Evidence: `s548` at 2030.7s, [recording](https://medius.microsoft.com/Embed/video-nc/ebe58201-624e-4975-a2df-de2dd1ee154b), "very brief listening".
