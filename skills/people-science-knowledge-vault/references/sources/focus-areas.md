## Create Focus Areas in Viva Glint

`focus-areas` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/focus-areas-managers-creating

Full Focus Area creation, permissions, status, cascading, and FAQ guidance. Status is manually set, and cascades create independent one-time copies rather than synchronized plans.

1. **Role and program prerequisites** (documentation) - Creating/viewing Focus Areas requires Create focus areas and View dashboards and reports permissions, assignment to at least one program with Focus Areas enabled, and a program that is not deleted or disabled. Eligible users can work across all cycles of those programs.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L26`, "All of the following program conditions must be satisfied:".
2. **Score access and broader-team fallback** (documentation) - Users need access to score results to create a Focus Area. If their own group has too few respondents, enabled Broader Team Insights can support creation from the leader's results. A manager need not have been invited to the survey if required reporting permissions/team access exist.
   Topics: privacy-confidentiality, action-taking. Evidence: `L154`, "A: No. Users must have access to score results to create a Focus Area. If  Broader Team Insights  is enabled, users can create a Focus Area based on t".
3. **Company tab is not organization-wide** (documentation) - The Focus Areas Company tab shows the immediate work group, including manager/peers, counts and status tracker, not every organizational Focus Area even for a Service Admin. Use the Focus Area Overview report for broader review within access rights.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L44`, "The  Company  tab doesn't show every Focus Area across your organization, even if you're a Viva Glint Service Admin.".
4. **Prepare and choose one focus with the team** (documentation) - Review strengths, opportunities and suggested resources before a team conversation.    Topics: action-taking, feedback. Evidence: `L65`, "Save to your team's plan by selecting  Create Focus Area . You now see your new plan on the Focus Areas tab.".

Commit to Focus Area adds the plan to Small Steps Forward and updates Concierge completion and access links.

8. **Status is manually interpreted** (documentation) - Users manually set Not Done, Off Track, On Track or Complete; survey close does not update status automatically. A manager may mark Complete because the team is ready to move on even if the original work is not finished, so completion is not an automatic objective achievement measure.

9. **Track due dates, tasks and discussion** (documentation) - Managers can change target completion dates, mark action items complete, note progress/roadblocks and tag people for input. Focus Areas can be reordered to prioritize attention.
   Topics: action-taking, feedback. Evidence: `L90`, "Specifies the target completion date for the Focus Area. Clicking on this date opens a date picker, allowing the manager to change it if necessary.".
10. **Cascading scope and sender access** (documentation) - Cascading normally targets direct reports in the manager hierarchy; Focus Area Overview report access can extend scope to teams visible there. The sender needs Create focus areas and View dashboard and reports permissions.

11. **Engagement and 360 recipients differ** (documentation) - Recipients need Create focus areas permission. For engagement cascades their role must be associated with the originating program; for 360 cascades they must be a subject in the originating 360 cycle.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking, feedback. Evidence: `L101`, "The user receiving the cascaded Focus Area needs to be assigned to a role with the  Create focus areas  permission.".
12. **Recipients without reporting permission** (documentation) - Recipients without View dashboard and reports cannot create their own Focus Areas but can receive and edit cascaded ones, visible only on the Focus Area dashboard. Recipients with report permission see the originating cycle in its dropdown.

13. **Cascades are independent one-time copies** (documentation) - Every cascade creates a separate copy even if the recipient already has that Focus Area. Copies are not merged or replaced and subsequent changes/completion do not synchronize with the original or other copies.

14. **Cascading cannot be turned off** (documentation) - The FAQ states cascading is available to users permitted to create Focus Areas and cannot be disabled. Actual recipients remain subject to hierarchy/report-access and program/360-cycle eligibility rules.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L144`, "A: Any user who has permission to create a Focus Area can cascade one. This functionality can’t be turned off.".

They are not simply a lowest-score list or proof that a suggested intervention will work.

17. **New-cycle reporting needs a new Focus Area** (documentation) - Create a new Focus Area for each survey cycle to include it in that cycle's reporting, even when continuing the same action; actions do not carry over automatically. 360 feedback can also support Focus Areas when enabled.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L138`, "A: Yes. To include a Focus Area in reporting for a new survey cycle, you must create a new Focus Area for that cycle.".
