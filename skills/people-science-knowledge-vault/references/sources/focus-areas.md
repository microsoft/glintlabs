## Create Focus Areas in Viva Glint

`focus-areas` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/focus-areas-managers-creating

Full Focus Area creation, permissions, status, cascading, and FAQ guidance. Status is manually set, and cascades create independent one-time copies rather than synchronized plans.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Permissions and program prerequisites** (documentation) - Creating or viewing Focus Areas depends on role permissions, program assignment, enabled features, and access to results.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L9`, "program conditions".
2. **One collaborative focus** (recommendation) - Review strengths, opportunities, and resources with the team, then concentrate on one manageable priority.
   Topics: action-taking, feedback. Evidence: `L27`, "one Focus Area".
3. **Local versus organization-wide visibility** (documentation) - The Company tab shows the immediate work group, while organization-level review uses the Focus Area Overview report.
   Topics: glint-admin, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L40`, "doesn't show every".
4. **Guided conversation and presentation kit** (documentation) - Managers can use concierge guidance and interactive Team Conversations to discuss options and commit together.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, feedback. Evidence: `L79`, "facilitate a meaningful".
5. **Customizing actions and resources** (documentation) - Teams can inspect alternatives, add suggested actions or resources, and edit their plan.
   Topics: action-taking, glint-admin. Evidence: `L125`, "developing together".
6. **Manual progress tracking** (documentation) - Managers manually maintain status, due dates, completed items, progress comments, and roadblocks.
   Topics: action-taking, methods. Evidence: `L177`, "updated manually".
7. **Cascading access rules** (documentation) - Cascading depends on sender and recipient permissions, hierarchy/report access, and survey-specific conditions.
   Topics: glint-admin, leadership. Evidence: `L191`, "manager hierarchy".
8. **Independent copies, not synchronization** (documentation) - A cascaded Focus Area is a one-time separate copy; duplicates can exist and subsequent edits do not sync.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L235`, "changes aren't synchronized".
9. **Personalized recommendations** (documentation) - Suggested Focus Areas combine scores, comparisons, and relevance to the outcome rather than simply selecting the lowest item.
   Topics: survey-interpretation, action-taking. Evidence: `L241`, "scores and drivers".
10. **Cycle-specific reporting and continuity** (documentation) - Continuing an action in a new cycle requires creating a new Focus Area for that cycle; plans do not automatically carry over.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L263`, "carry over automatically".
