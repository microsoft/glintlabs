## Customize Suggested Action templates in Viva Glint

`customize-suggested-action-templates-in-viva-glint` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/glint/setup/customize-action-plans

Full Content Resources and Suggested Action Template administration guide. Updates propagate differently to existing Focus Areas depending on state and access path.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Incremental team-approved action** (framework) - Suggested resources support manageable improvements within a team-agreed Focus Area.
   Topics: action-taking, manager-effectiveness. Evidence: `L4`, "incremental improvements".
2. **Driver-to-template structure** (documentation) - Survey items map to Suggested Action Templates, which contain action items and linked resources.
   Topics: action-taking, glint-admin. Evidence: `L13`, "associated with a survey item".
3. **Content library prerequisite** (documentation) - Resources must exist in Content Resources before they can be attached to a suggested action template.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L2`, "must first exist".
4. **Standard and customer content** (documentation) - The library combines prepopulated material with customer-uploaded resources.
   Topics: learning-enablement, glint-admin. Evidence: `L23`, "upload".
5. **Multilingual publishing** (documentation) - New resources are added across program languages, with language-specific text editable separately.
   Topics: glint-admin, inclusion. Evidence: `L60`, "all program languages".
6. **External-link resource workflow** (documentation) - Create rich-text links with labels and descriptions, preview the destination, then save and publish.
   Topics: glint-admin, learning-enablement. Evidence: `L92`, "right page".
7. **Video resource preparation** (documentation) - Use a suitable embeddable video URL, create a video resource, and preview it before publication.
   Topics: glint-admin, learning-enablement. Evidence: `L154`, "video preview".
8. **Cloning and replacing suggested actions** (documentation) - Embed a video in a named cloned resource and replace the old suggested action in the Focus Area template.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L181`, "create a new name".
9. **State-dependent content propagation** (documentation) - Open, complete, missing, and deleted Focus Areas can retain or receive content differently; do not assume every edit changes existing plans.
   Topics: glint-admin, action-taking. Evidence: `L202`, "apply differently".
10. **Refresh and deprecation** (documentation) - Reselecting a Focus Area or using dashboard access can expose newer content; deprecation removes content across states after a new session.
   Topics: glint-admin. Evidence: `L230`, "regardless of status".
