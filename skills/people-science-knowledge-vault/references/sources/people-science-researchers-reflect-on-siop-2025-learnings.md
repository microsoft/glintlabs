## People Science Researchers Reflect on SIOP 2025 Learnings

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `low`.

**Source:** https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftvivablog/people-science-researchers-reflect-on-siop-2025-learnings/4403530

People Science practitioners' reflections on SIOP 2025, not a single new empirical study. Treat forecasts and conference consensus as commentary.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

1. **Human-in-the-loop AI** (commentary) - People Scientists advocate actively shaping AI with human oversight and ethical guardrails.
   Topics: ai-governance, people-science. Evidence: `L5`, "human in the loop".
2. **AI in talent workflows** (commentary) - Hiring, career development, and repeatable HR work are presented as potential AI applications.
   Topics: ai-agents, work-design. Evidence: `L5`, "hiring the right candidates".
3. **Scalable AI coaching** (hypothesis) - AI coaching could expand access to feedback, career planning, ideation, and skill development.
   Topics: ai-skills, feedback, growth-development. Evidence: `L8`, "scale".
4. **Empathy remains interpersonal** (commentary) - The authors distinguish scalable AI coaching from human support during vulnerability and emotional need.
   Topics: manager-effectiveness, wellbeing. Evidence: `L9`, "human empathy".
5. **Passive and non-sentiment signals** (commentary) - Broader data sources can reduce survey burden but are useful only insofar as they support better decisions.
   Topics: employee-listening, methods. Evidence: `L12`, "passive data".
6. **Insights returned to employees** (recommendation) - Useful listening insights should reach leaders, managers, and contributors rather than remain within HR.
   Topics: employee-listening, action-taking. Evidence: `L12`, "shared back".
7. **Safety during workplace change** (commentary) - Psychological safety is presented as an enabling condition for honest feedback, experimentation, and adaptation.
   Topics: psychological-safety, change-management. Evidence: `L15`, "speaking up".
8. **Established adoption frameworks** (recommendation) - Technology Acceptance Model and social-learning concepts can structure AI adoption work.
   Topics: ai-adoption, people-science. Evidence: `L19`, "Technology Acceptance Model".
9. **Small experiments before scaling** (recommendation) - Test a few everyday AI use cases and iterate to learn where deeper investment is worthwhile.
   Topics: ai-adoption, action-taking. Evidence: `L20`, "start small".
10. **Cross-disciplinary People Science skills** (recommendation) - The authors call for greater AI literacy and collaboration with IT, product, and design.
   Topics: hr-it, ai-skills. Evidence: `L21`, "evolve our skillsets".
