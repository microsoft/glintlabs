## The state of AI change readiness

`ai-change-readiness` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `top5`.

**Source:** https://adoption.microsoft.com/files/viva/The-state-of-AI-change-readiness-eBook.pdf

All 24 pages reviewed. April 2024 online panel of 1,800 full-time employees in nine markets and organizations of 1,000+; profiles use n=1,389. Self-report, observational findings. HPO membership is respondent-rated; variance explained by a model is not a causal effect. Reused in the profiles and HR/IT decks and multiple 2024-25 drops.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete extracted text of the public 24-page PDF. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

**Study-family links:** ai-readiness-april-2024

1. **Employee-led adoption and BYOAI** (finding) - AI use often precedes company rollout, creating energy to harness and risks from unsupported tools.
   Topics: ai-adoption, ai-governance. Evidence: `p5`, "67%".
2. **Change saturation and burnout** (finding) - More burnout indicators coincide with greater change stress and less perceived capacity to keep up.
   Topics: wellbeing, change-management. Evidence: `p7`, "3+ burnout".
3. **Engagement and realized individual value** (finding) - Engaged respondents report more use, support, and individual benefits; the proposed virtuous cycle is a hypothesis, not a proven causal loop.
   Topics: engagement, ai-value. Evidence: `p9`, "19 percentage points".
4. **Five facets of AI readiness** (framework) - Readiness combines understanding, motivation, skills, opportunity, and perceived value; support all five rather than measuring licenses alone.
   Topics: ai-adoption, ai-skills. Evidence: `p10`, "opportunity".
5. **Leader-contributor change gap** (finding) - Communication, learning access, and involvement in decisions differ sharply across levels, warning against assuming readiness.
   Topics: leadership, change-management. Evidence: `p11`, "44%".
6. **People-centric high performance** (finding) - HPO respondents describe more respectful, inclusive, supportive change experiences and stronger links between personal and organizational AI value.
   Topics: leadership, inclusion. Evidence: `p13`, "people-centric".
7. **Sponsored access and experimentation** (finding) - HPO respondents report greater company-provided access and opportunities to experiment; the comparison does not isolate a tooling effect.
   Topics: ai-adoption, ai-value. Evidence: `p15`, "83%".
8. **Role-specific, agile co-creation** (recommendation) - Combine informal learning with structured workflow experiments and adjust direction using employee feedback.
   Topics: work-design, change-management. Evidence: `p18`, "Co-build".
9. **Champion communities and differentiated needs** (recommendation) - The Microsoft champion example and five sentiment profiles illustrate scalable peer support tailored to different starting points, not fixed employee labels.
   Topics: learning-enablement, ai-skills. Evidence: `p20`, "five".
10. **Concerns and responsible transformation** (recommendation) - Address security, overreliance, job loss, and deskilling with empathy, human safeguards, cross-disciplinary ownership, and ongoing listening.
   Topics: ai-governance, hr-it. Evidence: `p21`, "Over-reliance".
