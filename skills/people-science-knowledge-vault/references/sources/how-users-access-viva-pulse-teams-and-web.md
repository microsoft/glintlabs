## How users access Viva Pulse (Teams & web)

`how-users-access-viva-pulse-teams-and-web` | catalog | reviewed

**Priority:** Original vault; retained tier `medium`.

**Source:** https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/viva/pulse/setup-admin-access/access-pulse-via-teams-or-web

Short access procedure, not research. Themes retain installation/access prerequisites and Teams/web entry requirements; listed browsers are recommendations, not separately validated capabilities.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete freshly retrieved public Microsoft Learn article text reviewed, including substantive tables. Sign-in notices and page chrome excluded as evidence. Linked pages, courses, downloads, recordings and image-only details were not reviewed; no refreshed comprehensive figure or media review is claimed.

1. **Installation and granted-access prerequisite** (documentation) - Users access Pulse after it has been installed and access granted. The page describes Teams and web entry points, not the complete administrator installation procedure.
   Topics: pulse-admin. Evidence: `L19`, "Once you've  installed Viva Pulse and granted access to users , users can access Pulse in the Microsoft Teams app or as a web app.".
2. **Teams entry and cross-client availability** (documentation) - Users with Microsoft Teams licenses can find Viva Pulse in the Teams app menu and select Add; this adds it across Teams clients including mobile. Pinning the app is optional for repeated access.
   Topics: pulse-admin. Evidence: `L24`, "Select the  Viva Pulse  app, and then select  Add . This will add the app to all your Teams clients, including mobile.".
3. **Web URL and recommended browsers** (documentation) - Use https://Pulse.Viva.Cloud.Microsoft/ for browser access. Recommended browsers are Edge, Chrome and Firefox, plus Safari on iOS and Chrome on Android; the page does not establish additional browser feature parity or license exemptions.
   Topics: pulse-admin. Evidence: `L27`, "To access Viva Pulse in a browser, users can go to  https://Pulse.Viva.Cloud.Microsoft/ .".
