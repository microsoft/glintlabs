## Research Drop:  Diving into AI Transformation Profiles to Understand Unique Needs Across Employee Personas

`research-drop-4336280` | research-drop | reviewed

**Priority:** New catalog; before relevant original-vault resources.

**Source:** https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoftvivablog/research-drop-diving-into-ai-transformation-profiles-to-understand-unique-needs-/4336280

November 2024 public article and four figures reviewed. Same April 2024 AI-readiness study as the readiness eBook and profiles deck, not independent replication. Latent profiles n=1,389; user-only value comparisons exclude nonusers and have small skeptic/pessimist subsets.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Also reviewed OCR of 4 public figures; numeric claims cross-checked against written text where available. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

**Study-family links:** ai-readiness-april-2024

1. **Profiles combine three experiences** (method) - Engagement, past change experience, and AI sentiment jointly distinguish five study-specific profiles.
   Topics: ai-adoption, methods. Evidence: `L8`, "latent profile analysis".
2. **An encouraging but sample-specific mix** (finding) - Over 70% belong to the Multiplier or Advocate groups in this sample; these are not universal workforce proportions.
   Topics: ai-adoption, engagement. Evidence: `L26`, "70%".
3. **Adapt profiles to local context** (recommendation) - Culture, job security, innovation, leadership, and other local experiences may change the relevant distinctions.
   Topics: change-management, inclusion. Evidence: `L31`, "other considerations".
4. **Usage frequency differs across profiles** (finding) - Multipliers have the highest use, while nonuse is more common among AI Skeptics and Change Pessimists.
   Topics: ai-adoption. Evidence: `L45`, "consistent downward pattern".
5. **Value is not identical to adoption** (framework) - RIVA examines recognized benefits among users, adding context beyond usage counts.
   Topics: ai-value, survey-interpretation. Evidence: `L49`, "actively recognize".
6. **Value comparisons have selection limits** (finding) - Only small shares of skeptics and pessimists use AI, limiting generalization of their user-only RIVA scores.
   Topics: methods, ai-value. Evidence: `L52`, "small subset".
7. **Quick wins precede less tangible benefits** (finding) - Task-speed benefits are more common than stress or decision benefits in the less enthusiastic groups.
   Topics: ai-value, wellbeing. Evidence: `L53`, "less tangible".
8. **Skepticism and change fatigue differ** (finding) - AI Skeptics and Change Pessimists can require different support and recognize different kinds of value.
   Topics: change-management, ai-adoption. Evidence: `L56`, "not the same".
9. **Change conditions, not employee identity** (recommendation) - Profiles can shift when environments and experiences change; do not use them as fixed labels.
   Topics: inclusion, change-management. Evidence: `L59`, "not static".
10. **Every profile can contribute** (recommendation) - Use champions' energy and hesitant employees' feedback to create a more inclusive rollout with clear communication and continuous listening.
   Topics: feedback, learning-enablement. Evidence: `L60`, "all types of profiles".

   Topics: leadership, ai-adoption. Evidence: `L22`, "50%".

   Topics: feedback, action-taking. Evidence: `L58`, "visibly".
10. **Connect social nuance with organizational memory** (recommendation) - Combine recurring surveys and pulses with everyday team dialogue rather than replacing one with the other.
   Topics: employee-listening, action-taking. Evidence: `L59`, "organizational memory".

March 2026 public article and four figures reviewed. July 2025 panel n=1,800 with contextual cited literature. The four-I leadership mapping is practical guidance; cross-sectional support, modeling, and value comparisons are not causal leadership-intervention effects.

**Reviewed asset scope:** Complete published article/documentation body. Also reviewed OCR of 4 public figures; numeric claims cross-checked against written text where available. Linked courses and other resources are separate assets, not automatically reviewed. No claim of complete linked-recording coverage.

**Study-family links:** agentic-teaming-trust-july-2025

1. **Human leadership remains necessary** (framework) - AI changes how leadership is expressed, not employees' need for clarity, guidance, and support.
   Topics: leadership, change-management. Evidence: `L11`, "human side".
2. **Perceived support and frequent use** (finding) - Employees who feel supported report more daily agent use, but support gaps persist across levels.
   Topics: ai-adoption, leadership. Evidence: `L9`, "1.8x".
3. **Four transformational-leadership dimensions** (framework) - Intellectual stimulation, individualized consideration, inspirational motivation, and idealized influence provide the organizing framework.
   Topics: leadership, people-science. Evidence: `L18`, "four core".
4. **Model questioning instead of supplying answers** (recommendation) - Intellectual stimulation means scrutinizing outputs, probing assumptions, and preserving judgment rather than accepting plausible AI answers.
   Topics: ai-governance, ai-skills. Evidence: `L37`, "question".
5. **Make expectations and human review explicit** (recommendation) - Define good AI use, non-negotiable review, override situations, and reflective questions that build critical habits.
